<TabItem value="question">

Bob could be Bluffed by Alice's clue. Before blind-playing, Bob sees a very strong Finesse he could give to another player. Is deferring his possible Bluff categorically forbidden in the same way as deferring a suspected Double Bluff?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No, but it is risky and should be used sparingly.** A possible single Bluff may sometimes be deferred in order to perform a Finesse, although doing so can cause Information Desynchronization and can even break an unseen Double Bluff. Deferring a Bluff to perform another Bluff is much worse and should almost never be done. By contrast, once a Double Bluff is suspected, either reacting blind-player must never defer.

</TabItem>

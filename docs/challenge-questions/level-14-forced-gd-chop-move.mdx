<TabItem value="question">

Bob is forced to discard his clued card to cover a possible Reverse Trash Finesse, but the card is revealed to be the genuinely playable matching card. The discard is therefore a Gentleman's Discard rather than actual trash.

Do the cards to the right of Bob's discarded card remain untouched?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** This is the Forced Gentleman's Discard Chop Move. Even though the revealed card makes Bob's action a Gentleman's Discard, the discard was forced by a shape that looked like a Reverse Trash Finesse, so the cards to its right are Chop Moved exactly as they would have been in the trash case.

</TabItem>

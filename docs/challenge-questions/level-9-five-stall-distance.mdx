<TabItem value="question">

Alice is in a valid Early Game Stalling Situation and the only remaining required action is a 5 Stall. Bob has an off-chop 5 one card away from chop. Cathy has an off-chop 5 two cards away from chop. Bob also has two known playable cards.

Which 5 should Alice clue?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice must clue **Bob's 5**. Level 9 requires a 5 Stall to target the 5 closest to chop. Bob having playable cards does not reverse this ordering; in the source example, the closer 5 is chosen even when its owner has two playable cards.

<TabItem value="question">

Alice gives Bob a Play Clue that could be either a normal clue on the final playable red 2 or a Reverse Trash Finesse through Cathy's red 2. One copy of red 2 is already discarded, so Cathy's visible red 2 is critical. Should Bob still cover both interpretations by discarding his clued 2?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A Reverse Trash Finesse must contain a potential Gentleman's Discard line. With one red 2 already discarded, Cathy's remaining red 2 is critical, so Bob cannot legally cover the playable interpretation by performing a Gentleman's Discard of the other red 2. Therefore the Reverse Trash Finesse interpretation is unavailable; the team must interpret the clue some other way instead of forcing Cathy's critical card to blind-play.

</TabItem>

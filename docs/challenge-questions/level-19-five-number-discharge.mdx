All 1s are played. Alice gives an otherwise-illegal 5 Stall to Cathy's slot-1 5, and Cathy has trash red 1 immediately to its right. What does Bob play, and how should the table treat Cathy's red 1 afterward?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob plays his **Third Finesse Position** as a **5 Number Discharge**. Cathy's adjacent red 1 is then marked as **globally known trash**. It is treated as positively clued for conventions such as Trash Chop Move, so Cathy's Finesse Position moves past it to the next unclued card.

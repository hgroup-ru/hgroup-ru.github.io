<TabItem value="question">

Alice is in a Stalling Situation and sees an off-chop 5 that could receive a 5 Stall. However, Alice also knows that one of her own cards is immediately playable. There is no special Finesse Position Exception involved.

May Alice give the 5 Stall instead of playing her card?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A 5 Stall is a last resort. The player giving it must have no immediately playable card, no known safe discard, and no other visible normal Play Clue or Save Clue. This last-resort rule applies in both Early Game and Mid-Game Stalling Situations, except for the narrow Finesse Position Exception described separately.

</TabItem>

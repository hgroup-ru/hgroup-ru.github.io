<TabItem value="question">

Alice is in a Stalling Situation. Bob has an untouched off-chop green 5, and Alice has no normal Play Clue or Save Clue to give. However, Alice knows that her own slot 1 card is the immediately playable red 2.

Should Alice clue Bob's 5 as a stall or play her red 2?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice should **play the red 2**. A 5 Stall is a last-resort action: the cluer must not already have an immediately playable card or known safe discard available. The fact that the table is in a Stalling Situation does not turn the off-chop 5 into a better action than Alice's known play.

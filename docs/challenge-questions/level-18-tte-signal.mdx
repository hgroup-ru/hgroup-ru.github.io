Red 3 and all other 1s are played. Bob holds red 4, red 1, blue 1, green 1, yellow 1. Alice could clue number 4 cleanly, but instead clues red, touching the playable red 4 and trash red 1. Cathy sees no red 5 in either teammate's hand, and red 5 is the single missing useful red identity. What special message does the avoidable trash touch carry?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

It is **Trash Touch Elimination** for the single missing useful red card, red 5. Because Alice deliberately chose a Good-Touch-violating red clue despite a clean number-4 clue being available, the extra touch is meaningful. Cathy should place red-5 Elimination Notes on her hand.

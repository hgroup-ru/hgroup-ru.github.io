At 0 clues, Alice has a known playable card but deliberately discards instead. Bob, the next player, already has his own known playable red 2. Cathy has a critical yellow 5 on chop.

If Alice's discard is a genuine last-resort Scream, whose chop moves?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Bob and Cathy both Chop Move.** Bob already has a safe action, so a last-resort Scream aimed only at him would make no sense. The Scream therefore echoes through Bob and reaches Cathy, producing an **Echo Scream Discard Chop Move**. Both Chop Moved players are forbidden from discarding the moved chop on their next turn.

<TabItem value="question">

Alice makes what would normally be a last-resort Scream Discard. Bob, the next player, already has either a known playable card or a known safe trash discard. What does Alice's discard mean at Level 22, and which players are prevented from discarding their chop on their next turn?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Because Bob already has a safe action, an ordinary Scream aimed only at Bob would not be a last resort. The discard therefore becomes an **Echo Scream Discard Chop Move**: it bounces off Bob and Chop Moves both Bob and the player after him. As with a normal Scream Discard, **every player who was Chop Moved may not discard that chop on their next turn**.

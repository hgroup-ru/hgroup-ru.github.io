<TabItem value="question">

Alice uses a complicated move to get Bob's playable red 3 even though she could have given Bob a direct red Play Clue. However, the complicated clue also touches a second useful card in Cathy's hand and gives that card positive information that the direct clue would not provide.

Should the team treat Alice's move as Unnecessary and add the usual extra effect?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A complicated move is only Unnecessary when it has no side benefit. Picking up an additional useful card is a genuine benefit, so the move is **Necessary** for Level 24 purposes even though the main playable card could have been clued directly. The team does not add an Unnecessary-move extra effect.

</TabItem>

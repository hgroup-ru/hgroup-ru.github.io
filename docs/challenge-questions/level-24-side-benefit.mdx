<TabItem value="question">

Alice could directly Play Clue a target card, but instead uses a complicated advanced move that also picks up another useful card and fixes an impending Good Touch problem. Is the move automatically Unnecessary merely because a direct Play Clue existed?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A move is only Unnecessary when the complicated route could be replaced by a direct Play Clue **without losing a meaningful side benefit**. Picking up useful cards, fixing an impending misplay/Good Touch violation, or positively filling in existing touched cards can make the advanced move Necessary. Negative-information fill-in alone does not count for this simplification.

</TabItem>

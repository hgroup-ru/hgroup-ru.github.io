<TabItem value="question">

Red is stacked through 3 and there are 0 clues. Alice has two clued red cards that she has identified as `r4` and `r5`. Bob's slot 1 is the other `r4`; his `b2` is on chop, while his `b5` already has a 5 clue. Cathy's untouched `y5` is on chop.

How should Alice move?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice should **Gentleman's Discard her known `r4`**.

1. The discard creates one clue.
2. Bob plays the transferred `r4`.
3. Cathy gives Bob a blue Play Clue on `b2`; the already-5-clued `b5` is filled in by the same clue.
4. Alice's known `r5` is now playable; she plays it and regains a clue token.
5. Bob uses that token to 5 Save Cathy's `y5`.

A raw Generation Discard of an unknown chop card would also buy a token, but it needlessly risks an unknown card and gives up the useful transfer line.

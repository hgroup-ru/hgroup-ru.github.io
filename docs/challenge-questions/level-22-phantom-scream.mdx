<TabItem value="question">

A Phantom Playable card is about to be discarded. What factors does Level 22 require the team to consider before using a Scream Discard to protect it? How should the team think about a tricky case where the Phantom Playable is a 4?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

The team should ask whether the meaning will be clear, whether the in-between cards will be clued immediately, whether other critical chops need saving now or soon, and whether the Phantom Playable is a 3 or a 4. In a tricky or tight position, it is better to let the Phantom Playable go, **especially if it is a 4**. The team should not bend over backwards for it, because every Scream Discard carries some risk that the discarded card itself was critical.

</TabItem>

<TabItem value="question">

Red 2 is played. Alice gives Bob a number 4 Play Clue touching one card. Bob sees no red 3 in Alice's or Cathy's hand, and he sees no other connecting card that could explain the clue.

Should Bob blind-play his own Finesse Position?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Yes.** Bob can infer that his clued 4 is intended to be the red 4 and that the missing red 3 must be in his own Finesse Position, so he blind-plays it. It turns out to be the blue 1 and plays successfully: the immediate mismatch reveals a legal **Self-Bluff**. At level 11 this works with a rank clue; a color Self-Bluff is not part of the level-11 convention.

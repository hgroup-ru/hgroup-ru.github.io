<TabItem value="question">

Alice has a known playable 4 that would lead into another known card in her own hand. She also has a known playable 5. The ordinary Priority table would normally favor the card that leads into Alice's own hand over a 5.

Does Alice have to play the 4 first?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** The **4's Priority Exception** reverses this specific comparison: the known playable **5 has Priority** over a known playable 4 that leads into the same player's own hand. The free clue and the strategic flexibility of the 5 justify the exception.

</TabItem>

<TabItem value="question">

Alice has two known playable cards. Blue 3 immediately leads into Bob's known blue 4 and would normally win the "play into someone else's hand" comparison. Red 3 instead advances the suit needed to make a useful card playable for Cathy, who is currently Locked. The table can clearly see that helping Cathy will unlock her.

Must Alice still choose blue 3 because it has the normal immediate-hand Priority?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A **Locked player is the explicit exception** to the simple "consider only the very next card" rule. When one line works toward unlocking Cathy, the team should prioritize that strategic need rather than mechanically following the ordinary immediate-hand comparison.

</TabItem>

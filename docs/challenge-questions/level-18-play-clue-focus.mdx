<TabItem value="question">

Alice has Elimination Notes for red 2 on several slots. Bob gives a number 2 clue touching slots 1, 3, and 4. The new information eliminates every red-2 possibility except slot 3. Is slot 1 still the Play Clue focus merely because it is the leftmost touched card?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** An Elimination Play Clue can single out the immediately playable Elimination card, so slot 3 becomes exactly red 2 and is the focus. The other touched 2s are collateral positive information and are not thereby promised playable. This special focus also cannot be stretched into a Delayed Play Clue, Prompt, or Finesse.

</TabItem>

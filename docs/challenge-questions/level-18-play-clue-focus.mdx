Red 1 and blue 1 are played. Alice has red-2 Elimination Notes on slots 2 through 5. Bob clues number 2, touching slots 1, 3, and 4. Which touched card is the Play Clue focus, and what is promised about the other touched 2s?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Slot 3 is exactly red 2 and is the focus.** Elimination rules out slot 1 as the red 2, so the clue singles out the playable Elimination card. Slots 1 and 4 can be any 2s and are not promised playable. An Elimination Play Clue is not a Delayed Play Clue, Prompt, or Finesse.

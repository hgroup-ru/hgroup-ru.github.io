<TabItem value="question">

Alice makes the flowchart's emergency discard. Bob has no known play or known safe discard. Cathy has a known play or known safe discard. How should Bob interpret Alice's action? What restriction applies to Bob after he reacts?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

The flowchart identifies an ordinary **Scream Discard Chop Move**. Bob has the important chop, so he permanently Chop Moves by one card. He is then **not allowed to discard on this turn**. Cathy's already safe action is why Alice's discard need not be interpreted as a Generation Discard for Cathy.

<TabItem value="question">

Alice had a known play at 0 clues but made an emergency discard instead. Bob has no known play or known safe discard and has a critical yellow 5 on chop. Cathy, however, already has a known playable green 2.

How should Bob interpret Alice's discard, and may he simply discard after reacting?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

This is an ordinary **Scream Discard Chop Move** aimed at Bob. Bob permanently Chop Moves his critical yellow 5. He is then **not allowed to discard on this turn**; if he has nothing productive to do, he must spend the generated clue rather than risk a second critical card immediately behind the moved one. Cathy's existing safe play is why Alice does not need the discard to generate an action for Cathy.

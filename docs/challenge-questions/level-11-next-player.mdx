<TabItem value="question">

The turn order is Alice → Bob → Cathy → Donald. All 2s are played. Alice could clue Donald red, touching his red 4. Cathy has a playable blue 3 in her Finesse Position.

Can Alice give this clue intending it to be a Bluff on Cathy?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Alice is in Bluff Seat for Bob, not Cathy. A Bluff may only make the very next player blind-play on the very next turn. Alice cannot deliberately skip Bob and use Cathy's later blind-play as the reveal of a Bluff; that would leave misinformation unresolved and violate the Bluff Seat / Lie Principle restrictions.

</TabItem>

<TabItem value="question">

Alice clues one or more newly touched cards that everyone knows are trash. The team can rule out a Trash Chop Move, and it can also rule out both Trash Push and Trash Push Finesse. What interpretation remains? If a Bad Trash Chop Move Ejection is technically imaginable, which interpretation has priority?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

The clue communicates a **Trash Double Ignition**. Known-trash clues first have to be checked against the ordinary Trash Chop Move and Trash Push families; when neither can explain the clue, TDI is the interpretation. Players should prefer Trash Double Ignition over a **Bad Trash Chop Move Ejection**, which Level 21 explicitly treats as very rare and later material.

Red 3 and blue 1 are played. Bob's hand is red 4, red 3, blue 4, blue 4, blue 1. Alice must clue red 4 now. A red clue duplicates red 3, while a number-4 clue duplicates blue 4s. Alice chooses number 4. Should the team infer Trash Touch Elimination from the bad touch?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Both available clue types violate Good Touch Principle, so Alice is forced to choose the lesser evil. Trash Touch Elimination requires a meaningful contrast with a clean alternative; a bad touch that could not reasonably be avoided is interpreted normally.

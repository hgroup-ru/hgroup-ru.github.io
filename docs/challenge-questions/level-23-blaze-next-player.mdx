<TabItem value="question">

Alice makes a weird Gentleman's Discard in a non-emergency position. The matching card is off Cathy's Finesse Position, but Bob—the very next player—cannot safely blind-play the corresponding Finesse Position. Donald, who acts after Cathy, could demonstrate that position cleanly.

Can Alice intend a Blaze Discard that waits for Donald to perform the blind-play later?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A Blaze Discard must be demonstrated by the **very next player**. Bob has to react immediately to prove the matching card's Finesse Position. A later player cannot rescue an otherwise unreadable Blaze line; if Bob cannot make the required immediate play, Alice cannot rely on Donald to turn the discard into a valid Blaze.

</TabItem>

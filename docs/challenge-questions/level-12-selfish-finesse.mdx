<TabItem value="question">

Nothing is played. Bob has a globally known red 1. Instead of playing it, Bob gives Cathy a red Play Clue on her red 3. Alice can see that Bob knows his red 1, and Alice's own Finesse Position is still completely unclued.

What should Alice infer about her Finesse Position, and should she play it immediately?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice should mark her Finesse Position as **red 2**, but she should **not play it yet**. Bob's clue is a Selfish Finesse: he delayed his known red 1 to lock in the Finesse before Alice's Finesse Position could move. The players involved mark the connection and wait patiently for Bob to play red 1; only then does Alice blind-play red 2.

Nothing is played. Alice gives Cathy a blue Play Clue that would normally require Bob to connect with blue 1. Bob has negative blue information on every card in his hand and sees no better conventional explanation. Should he refuse to blind-play?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Bob knows that the expected Finesse identity is impossible, and that impossibility is the signal. With no better explanation, he blind-plays his Finesse Position as a **Known Bluff** instead of pretending that he might hold blue 1.

<TabItem value="question">

It is the Mid-Game and Alice starts her turn with 8 clues. She has no normal Play Clue or Save Clue and no 5 Stall. She can either give a Tempo Clue that re-clues a card to make it play, give a Fill-In Clue, or make an 8 Clue Save.

Which class of clue has priority?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice must prefer the **Tempo Clue Stall**. At 8 clues, the Stall Table allows all three options, but their precedence is not tied: Tempo Clue Stall is precedence 3, while Fill-In Clue and 8 Clue Save are precedence 4. A lower-precedence stall is not available merely because it is also permitted by the current Stalling Situation.

</TabItem>

<TabItem value="question">

It is the Mid-Game and Alice starts her turn with 8 clues. Alice has no known playable card, no normal Play Clue or Save Clue, and no 5 Stall. Bob's blue 2 can be re-clued to make it play, Cathy's already-clued 3 can receive a Fill-In Clue, and Donald has an off-slot-1 card that could receive an 8 Clue Save.

Which clue should Alice give?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice should **re-clue Bob's blue 2 as a Tempo Clue Stall**. At 8 clues, the Stall Table allows all three options, but their precedence is not tied: Tempo Clue Stall is precedence 3, while Fill-In Clue and 8 Clue Save are precedence 4. Alice cannot choose a lower-precedence stall merely because it is also permitted by the current Stalling Situation.

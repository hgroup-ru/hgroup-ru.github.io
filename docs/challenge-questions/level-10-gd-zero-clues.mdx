<TabItem value="question">

All 1s are played and there are 0 clues. Alice knows her red card is `r2`. Bob's slot 1 is the other `r2`, and Bob has an untouched `y5` on chop.

How should Alice move?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice should **Gentleman's Discard the `r2`**. The discard generates a clue, Bob plays the transferred `r2`, and Cathy can use the generated clue to 5 Save Bob's untouched `y5` before his next turn. This is not a Scream Discard: a less dangerous special-discard line exists, and Scream is a last resort.

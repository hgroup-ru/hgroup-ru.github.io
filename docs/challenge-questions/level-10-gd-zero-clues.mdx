<TabItem value="question">

All 1s are played and there are 0 clues. Alice knows her red card is `r2`. The other `r2` is in Bob's Finesse Position, and Bob has an untouched `y5` on chop.

How should Alice move?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice should **Gentleman's Discard the `r2`**. The discard generates a clue, Bob plays the transferred `r2`, and Cathy can use the generated clue to 5 Save Bob's untouched `y5` before his next turn. This line is safer than a Scream Discard, and in this position a Scream Discard is not legal anyway: Scream is a last resort and cannot be used when a less dangerous way to save the card exists.

<TabItem value="question">

Cathy has a re-clued red card whose possibilities before the clue sequence are {red 2, red 3}. The re-clue adds no new positive clue mark, but after the sequence Cathy can narrow the card to exactly red 3. Is this formally a No-Information clue?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A No-Information clue must satisfy **both** conditions: it gives no additional positive information, **and the elements of the card's superposition are the same before and after the clue sequence**. Here the superposition shrinks, so the clue conveyed valuable information even without a new visible mark.

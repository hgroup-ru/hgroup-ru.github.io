<TabItem value="question">

A reacting player has ruled out a direct Play Clue and a Prompt. Two interpretations remain conventionally legal and fit all visible information: one is a Bluff that requires exactly one blind-play, and the other is a Finesse that requires two blind-plays. No later action is needed to distinguish them before the reaction.

Which interpretation should the reacting player choose?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

The player should assume the **one-blind-play Bluff**. Under Occam's Razor, after simpler direct and Prompt interpretations have been ruled out, players prefer the interpretation requiring fewer blind-plays. This does not make an otherwise illegal Bluff legal; the premise here explicitly says both remaining interpretations are valid.

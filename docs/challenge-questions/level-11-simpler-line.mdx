<TabItem value="question">

Alice gives Cathy a strange Play Clue. Bob is the reacting player: if he simply ignores the clue, Cathy will eventually misplay. Bob can rule out a direct Play Clue and a Prompt. He can make the remaining cards fit in two legal ways: a Bluff in which only Bob blind-plays, or a Finesse line that requires Bob and then another player to blind-play. Nobody will act before Bob has to choose.

Which line should Bob follow?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob should follow the **one-blind-play Bluff**. Once direct and Prompt interpretations are impossible and Bob cannot wait for new information, Occam's Razor tells the reacting player to choose the interpretation with fewer blind-plays. The premise that both complete lines are legal matters: Occam's Razor chooses between valid interpretations; it never makes an otherwise illegal Bluff valid.

</TabItem>

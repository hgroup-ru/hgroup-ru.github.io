<TabItem value="question">

One blue 3 has been discarded, so Bob's blue 3 on chop is critical. Alice can save it with either blue or number 3. A number-3 clue would also re-touch Bob's known red 3, which duplicates Alice's globally known red 3. Alice chooses blue, touching only the critical blue 3. Should Bob treat that choice as a Suboptimal Prompt, Finesse, or Bluff?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** The blue clue has an ordinary **Good Touch Principle** justification: it avoids re-touching a known duplicate red 3. A Suboptimal move must be **clearly and meaningfully worse** than the available alternative with no ordinary explanation. Merely losing some apparent clue efficiency is not enough to manufacture extra Play Clue meaning.

<TabItem value="question">

Blue 1 is played. Alice has a globally known playable green 1; Bob has nothing useful to do. Alice clues number 2 to Cathy's slot 2, and Bob discards. Normally Cathy would read the clue as a Play Clue on blue 2. What extra interpretation must Cathy consider because Alice was Occupied?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Cathy must read it as an **Occupied Self-Finesse**. She blind-plays her Finesse Position; when it plays as red 1, the clued 2 is identified as the connecting red 2. The ordinary Play Clue has gained one extra step because the clue was Occupied.

It is the Early Game and Low Score Phase. Cathy has a 5 in slot 4, exactly one card away from chop, and Alice has another normal Play Clue available. Alice nevertheless clues Cathy's 5 by number. Is this merely an early 5 Stall?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Because Alice had something else useful to do, an ordinary Early-Game 5 Stall is not legal here. With the 5 exactly one-away from chop, the extra message is an **Early 5's Chop Move**: Cathy Chop Moves the card to the right of the 5.

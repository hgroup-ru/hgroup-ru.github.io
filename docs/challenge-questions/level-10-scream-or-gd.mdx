<TabItem value="question">

All 1s are played and there are 0 clues. Alice has a clued red card that she has fully identified as `r2`. Bob has the other `r2` in Finesse Position and a `y5` on chop. Cathy will be able to 5 Save Bob if the team can create a clue before her turn.

What should Alice do now: discard an unknown chop card as a Scream Discard, or use her known `r2` differently?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice should discard the known `r2` as a Gentleman's Discard. The discard creates a clue, Bob plays the transferred `r2` instead of discarding `y5`, and Cathy can then 5 Save the `y5`.

A Scream Discard is a last resort. Because this position has a less dangerous way to save Bob's chop, the emergency unknown discard is not the correct Scream line here.

<TabItem value="question">

Alice has a known playable card on slot 1 and known trash on slots 2 and 3. She wants to use a discard to Chop Move **Cathy**, not Bob. Which trash card should she discard?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Slot 3.** Discarding slot 2 would be the ordinary Shout Discard and Chop Move Bob. Skipping that expected trash card and discarding slot 3 combines Shout Discard with Trash Order, producing a **Shout Discard Order Chop Move** on Cathy.

<TabItem value="question">

Cathy's red 5 already has a positive number-5 clue. Alice now re-clues it red. Bob sees that reaching red 5 would normally require multiple blind-plays. Does the 5 Color Ejection interpretation apply?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** 5 Color Ejection only applies to a 5 with **no previous positive clues**. Re-cluing an already positively clued 5 signals the Finesse on the cards leading up to it. A Chop Move's invisible information would not block a 5CE, but this real number clue does.

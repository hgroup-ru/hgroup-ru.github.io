<TabItem value="question">

A 5 already has a positive clue. Alice now gives a color Play Clue that re-clues that 5. Bob would need several blind-plays to connect all the way to it. Should Bob use 5 Color Ejection because the chain is long?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** 5 Color Ejection only applies to a 5 with no prior positive clue. Re-cluing an already-clued 5 signals the ordinary Finesse interpretation through the cards leading up to the 5, even if that means multiple blind-plays. A prior Chop Move alone does not count as a positive clue and therefore would not disable 5 Color Ejection.

</TabItem>

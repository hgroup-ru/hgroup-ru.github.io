The game is in a clearly tempo-critical endgame with one card left in the deck. Alice is considering a number-2 clue to Bob's trash 2, intending Cathy to react through a Reverse Trash Finesse. Is that interpretation available here?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Level 14 explicitly turns **Reverse Trash Finesses off toward the end of the game** because they are slow and require extra discarding when the team needs tempo. Alice should not rely on the reverse interpretation in this endgame position.

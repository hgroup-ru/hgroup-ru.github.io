A clue has the shape of a possible Reverse Trash Finesse, but the game is near its end and the team urgently needs Tempo rather than extra discards.

Should the recipient still automatically entertain the reverse interpretation?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Reverse Trash Finesses depend on context and are specifically turned off toward the end of the game. They are slow and require additional discarding, so the team should not keep forcing the reverse interpretation when end-game Tempo makes that convention inappropriate.

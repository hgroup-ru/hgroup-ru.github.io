<TabItem value="question">

Alice gives a 5 Pull Finesse for Cathy's pulled red 2. Bob blind-plays green 1, unrelated to red 2. May Cathy interpret this as a special "5 Pull Bluff" and still play her pulled red 2?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** There is no 5 Pull Bluff. In a 5 Pull Finesse, the pulled card **always connects to the blind-play**. An unrelated green-1 blind-play cannot be used to sell Cathy's red 2 as the pulled continuation.

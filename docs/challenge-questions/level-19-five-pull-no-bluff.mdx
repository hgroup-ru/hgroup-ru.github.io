<TabItem value="question">

In the Low Score Phase, Alice gives Cathy a number-5 clue on the 5 in slot 1 while another normal Play Clue is available, so the clue cannot be a Stall. The card immediately to the right is red 2, one-away-from-playable. Bob blind-plays green 1, unrelated to red 2. May Cathy reinterpret this as a special "5 Pull Bluff" and still play her pulled red 2?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** There is no 5 Pull Bluff. In a 5 Pull Finesse, the pulled card **always connects to the blind-play**. An unrelated green-1 blind-play cannot be used to sell Cathy's red 2 as the pulled continuation.

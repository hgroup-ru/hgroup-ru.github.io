<TabItem value="question">

It is the first turn. Alice clues Bob's slot-1 5. From Bob's perspective Alice had no other Play or Save Clue except a possible 2 Save on Cathy's chop red 2. The clue could look like either a 5 Pull or a 5 Stall depending on whether another red 2 is hidden in Bob's hand. Which interpretation has priority, and what should Bob do?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob assumes **5 Stall**, not 5 Pull. When the only apparently ignored action is a 2 Save on chop, the simpler 5 Stall has special priority: Visible Rule says Alice could see another red 2 and therefore was not allowed to give that 2 Save. Bob writes red-2 Elimination Notes on his own hand and must not give the same 2 Save himself; he gives another clue or discards.

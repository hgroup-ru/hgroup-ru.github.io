<TabItem value="question">

Only red 1 is played. Cathy already has a 3 with a number clue and no color information. Alice gives Cathy a Play Clue to a new 4, then Bob blind-plays red 2. No stack is high enough for any 4 to be one-away-from-playable.

Should Cathy keep a simple Hard Bluff interpretation and wait?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A Hard Bluff requires a legal simple Bluff target to remain possible. Here no 4 is one-away-from-playable, so the new 4 cannot be an ordinary Bluff target. The truthful explanation is Finesse + Prompt: Cathy is promised red 3 and red 4, so she plays her unknown-color clued 3 as a Self-Prompt for red 3.

</TabItem>

<TabItem value="question">

Only red 1 is played. Cathy has an unknown-color 3 already clued by rank. Alice gives Cathy a Play Clue to a new 4, then Bob blind-plays red 2. No 4 is currently one-away-from-playable. Should Cathy keep a simple Bluff interpretation and wait?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A simple Bluff needs a legal Bluff target, but every 4 is at least two-away here. Cathy can therefore reject the Bluff interpretation. She is promised red 3 and red 4: she plays her clued 3 as a Self-Prompt for red 3, completing the Finesse + Prompt line.

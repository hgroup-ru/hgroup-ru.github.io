<TabItem value="question">

Blue 1 is played. Alice has a globally known yellow 1 and clues number 1 to Cathy, touching blue 1 on slot 3. Bob reads the Trash Finesse and blind-plays yellow 1, duplicating Alice's known card. No extra valuable cards were touched. Bob notices that his own duplicate blind-play was a 0-for-1. Should he therefore Time Travel Chop Move his own hand?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Blind-form Time Travel is unavailable when the clue looks like an **Assisted Trash Chop Move** on the clue receiver. This position has exactly that simpler explanation: Cathy Chop Moves the cards to the right of her clued blue 1. Bob does not add a second Chop Move on his own hand.

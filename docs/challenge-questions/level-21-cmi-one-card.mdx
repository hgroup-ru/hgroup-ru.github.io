<TabItem value="question">

On the first turn, Cathy's hand from newest to oldest is blue 4, blue 4, blue 5, red 1. Alice clues number 5 to Cathy, touching slot 3. Bob can see that Alice had ordinary Play Clues available and that Chop Moving Cathy's playable red 1 would otherwise be nonsensical. What must Bob do, and what does Cathy learn from his action?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob treats the strange Chop Move as a **Chop Move Ignition** and blind-plays his Finesse Position; in the source position it is blue 1. Bob's blind-play tells Cathy that the clue was not merely an Early 5's Chop Move. Cathy therefore knows the Chop Moved card is playable and blind-plays slot 4, the card immediately to the right of the clued 5.

<TabItem value="question">

Alice knows a clued red 1 in her hand. The other two copies of red 1 are simultaneously on Bob's and Cathy's Finesse Positions. May Alice discard her red 1 as a Gentleman's Discard and let the team infer which recipient should play?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Level 10 explicitly forbids a Gentleman's Discard of a 1 when the other two copies are on two different Finesse Positions at the same time. The transfer would not identify a unique recipient, so Alice must choose another action.

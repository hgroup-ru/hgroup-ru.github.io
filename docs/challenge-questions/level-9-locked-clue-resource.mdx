<TabItem value="question">

Cathy has a fully clued Locked Hand and acts immediately after Alice. The team has exactly 1 clue. Alice has no urgent normal clue to give and is considering spending the last clue on an unrelated low-value clue.

Is Alice free to spend it?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A Locked player cannot discard to generate a clue. Level 9 therefore protects the team's clue resource around a Locked Hand: at 0 clues another player must discard to generate one for the Locked player, and with only 1 clue left the team must not steal that last clue from them. Alice should preserve it rather than spend it on the unrelated low-value clue.

<TabItem value="question">

Bob has a Locked Hand and begins his turn with 0 clues. The team deliberately left him at 0 rather than spending a clue to re-clue one of his cards. Bob has two cards that appear equally likely to be playable.

What does the convention tell Bob to do?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob should make an **Anxiety Play**. Being deliberately left at 0 clues while Locked tells him that one of his cards is actually playable. He should play the card most likely to be playable; if more than one option is equally plausible, Leftmost Playable Principle selects the leftmost one.

</TabItem>

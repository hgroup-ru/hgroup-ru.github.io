<TabItem value="question">

Bob has a Locked Hand and begins his turn with 0 clues. The team deliberately left him at 0 rather than spending a clue to re-clue one of his cards. From Bob's notes, two cards are equally plausible candidates to be playable.

Which card should Bob play?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob should make an **Anxiety Play** and play the **leftmost of the two equally plausible candidates**. Being deliberately left at 0 clues while Locked tells him that one of his cards is actually playable. He first chooses the card most likely to be playable; when multiple options are equally plausible, Leftmost Playable Principle breaks the tie.

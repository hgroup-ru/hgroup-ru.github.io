<TabItem value="question">

Alice gives a clue that touches only cards that already have clues and adds new information to them. The team is in an allowed Double Discard Stalling Situation and no higher-precedence clue is available.

How should the clue be classified, and would the same shape have the same meaning in a normal situation?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Here it can be a **Fill-In Clue**: it touches only already-clued or Chop Moved cards and adds information that was not known before. But Fill-In Clues are special stalling tools. Outside an allowed Stalling Situation, a clue with this shape is not automatically a Fill-In Clue; it would normally be read as an ordinary Play Clue or according to the usual clue interpretation rules.

</TabItem>

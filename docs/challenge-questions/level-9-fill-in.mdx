<TabItem value="question">

Alice has just discarded a red 4, putting Bob in a Double Discard Stalling Situation. Bob has no higher-precedence clue available. Cathy already has a green clue on her green 3, and Bob could clue number 3, touching only that already-clued card and adding new information.

How should Bob classify that clue?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Here it can be a **Fill-In Clue**: it touches only already-clued or Chop Moved cards and adds information that was not known before.

The Stalling Situation is essential. Outside an allowed Stalling Situation, a clue with this shape is not automatically a Fill-In Clue; it would normally be read as an ordinary Play Clue or according to the usual clue interpretation rules.

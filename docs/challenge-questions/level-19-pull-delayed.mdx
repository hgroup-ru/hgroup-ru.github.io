<TabItem value="question">

A legal 5 Pull has identified the card immediately to the right of a 5. There are already touched cards that may need to play before the pulled card becomes playable. Must the receiver blind-play the pulled card on their very next turn anyway?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A 5 Pull is treated like a **Delayed Play Clue** (or potential Prompt), not like an ordinary Finesse requiring an immediate blind-play. The pulled card may have to wait for existing touched connections to resolve. This delayed behavior is one of the important differences between 5 Pull and Finesse/Bluff logic.

</TabItem>

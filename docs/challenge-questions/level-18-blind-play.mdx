<TabItem value="question">

Alice has Elimination Notes for red 4 on several cards. Later clues eliminate every noted slot except slot 5, but slot 5 still has no positive red or 4 clue.

May Alice play slot 5 without waiting for a direct clue?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Yes.** Once only one Elimination possibility remains, Alice knows the exact identity from the globally understood elimination history and may blind-play it as an **Elimination Blind-Play**. A positive clue is not required merely to repeat information the team already knows she can deduce.

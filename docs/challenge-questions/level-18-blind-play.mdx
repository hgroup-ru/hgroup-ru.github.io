Alice has red-4 Elimination Notes on several cards. A follow-up blue clue eliminates every candidate except slot 5. Red 4 is currently playable. Does Alice need another direct clue before playing slot 5?

<Question />

<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Once only one Elimination possibility remains, Alice knows the identity without a positive clue and can blind-play slot 5 as an **Elimination Blind-Play**.

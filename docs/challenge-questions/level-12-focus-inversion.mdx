<TabItem value="question">

Earlier, Alice gave Bob a number 1 clue that touched only his slot 1 card; Bob played it as the red 1. That old clue also gave every other card in Bob's hand negative 1 information. Cathy now gives Bob a blue Play Clue touching a new blue card in slot 1 and his blue chop card in slot 5.

Where is the focus of Cathy's clue?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

The focus is **slot 1**, not chop. Normally a new multi-touch Play Clue that includes chop would be chop-focused. But Bob already knows from the earlier number 1 clue that slot 5 cannot be the blue 1, so the chop card cannot be the currently playable blue card. Focus Inversion applies: the clue focuses the leftmost newly touched blue card, while the blue chop card can be blue 2, 3, 4, or 5 and is not promised playable now.

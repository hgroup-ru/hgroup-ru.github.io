<TabItem value="question">

Alice gives a Trash Finesse. Because of the current hand layout, the card Bob blind-plays could not actually have been obtained with a normal direct Play Clue.

Does that factual necessity prevent the Trash Finesse from receiving the Level 24 Unnecessary interpretation?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Trash Finesses and Trash Bluffs are a deliberate exception: they are **always treated as Unnecessary**, regardless of whether the blind-played card could really have been obtained with a normal clue. The team applies the appropriate extra effect anyway.

</TabItem>

<TabItem value="question">

Because of blocking, Alice genuinely could not directly clue Bob's blind-played connector. She uses a Trash Finesse to obtain it. Should the team say the move was Necessary and therefore suppress all Level 24 extra effects?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Trash Finesses and Trash Bluffs are a deliberate exception: the group always treats them as **Unnecessary**, regardless of whether the blind-played card could actually have been obtained by a normal clue. This simplification keeps their downstream Level 24 extra-message behavior stable.

</TabItem>

<TabItem value="question">

Alice gives Cathy a clue. Bob then blind-plays from Finesse Position, but Bob's card does not connect to Cathy's clued card. Also, Cathy's clued card does not need Bob's play to prove that it is playable. Can this still be an Ignition, and what distinguishes it from a Finesse and a Bluff?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Yes. An **Ignition** deliberately causes a blind-play from Finesse Position that is unrelated both to the clue itself and to the playability of the clued card. It therefore does not connect, unlike a Finesse, and nothing has to be proved to the clue-receiver, unlike both a Finesse and a Bluff.

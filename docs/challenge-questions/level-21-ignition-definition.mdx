<TabItem value="question">

Alice has just re-clued Cathy's globally known playable red 2 without touching any new cards. Alice has a known safe discard, so this is not a Stall. Bob reacts by blind-playing his Finesse Position card, which is green 2. All 1s are already played.

Does Bob's green 2 need to connect to Cathy's red 2, or prove that Cathy's card is playable, for the line to make sense?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Alice's otherwise useless re-clue is a Replay Double Ignition trigger. Bob's blind-play is an **Ignition**: it is deliberately unrelated to the clue and unrelated to the playability of Cathy's already-known red 2. That is the key distinction from a Finesse, where the blind-play connects, and from a Bluff, where the blind-play is needed to make the clued card look playable.

<TabItem value="question">

Bob blind-plays from Finesse Position because Alice intentionally triggered an Ignition. Must Bob's card connect to the clue target, or prove that the clued card is playable?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Neither.** An Ignition blind-play is deliberately unrelated both to the clue's identity and to the playability of the clued card. That separates it from a Finesse, which connects, and from a Bluff, whose unrelated play still communicates something about the clue target's playability.

</TabItem>

<TabItem value="question">

A color clue to an untouched 5 could be repaired as an Out-of-Order Finesse, but that repaired line would require Bob to blind-play **two** cards from his own hand. Which interpretation takes precedence?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**5 Color Ejection.** The Level 16 two-or-more-blind-plays threshold still takes precedence over the Level 20 Out-of-Order Finesse. If only one Bob blind-play were needed, Out-of-Order plus the Fix Clue could remain the intended line; with two or more, Bob uses Second Finesse Position as the Ejection.

</TabItem>

<TabItem value="question">

It is the Mid-Game with 8 clues. Alice gives Bob a yellow clue that touches his slot 1 yellow 2. Could Bob dismiss the clue as an arbitrary 8 Clue Save simply because Alice was forced to clue at 8?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** The 8 Clue Save special case may save any card on or off chop **except slot 1**. A clue that looks like an 8CS but lands on slot 1 is instead interpreted as a normal Play Clue or normal Save Clue. Here Bob must use the ordinary clue interpretation rather than treating the card as an arbitrary stall-save.

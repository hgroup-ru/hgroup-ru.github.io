<TabItem value="question">

There are 0 clues. Bob and Donald both have critical chop cards; Cathy has a safe discard. Alice and Bob each have a known playable card. Alice sees that if she plays, Bob will have to Generation Discard and lose his critical chop. What should Alice do, and how do Bob and Cathy interpret the same action differently?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice must **discard**. The same discard is simultaneously a **Scream Discard to Bob**, so Bob Chop Moves his critical card, and a **Generation Discard to Cathy**, so Cathy does **not** Chop Move. This is a **Composition Discard**: one discard composes two different emergency meanings for two different players.

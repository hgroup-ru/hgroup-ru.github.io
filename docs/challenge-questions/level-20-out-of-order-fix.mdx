<TabItem value="question">

Alice gives an Out-of-Order Play Clue whose initial focus is the later card in a same-suit sequence. Bob gives the required Fix Clue. After the receiver corrects the earlier identities, should they stop and treat the original focus as merely an error?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** In an Out-of-Order line, the original focus remains intentionally meaningful and is promised playable once the earlier sequence is resolved. The Fix Clue corrects the mistaken immediate identity; it does not erase Alice's original Play Clue. The receiver should play the earlier cards and then continue to the original focus.

</TabItem>

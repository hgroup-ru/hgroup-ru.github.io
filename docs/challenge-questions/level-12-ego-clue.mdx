<TabItem value="question">

Red 1 is played. Alice knows she has a playable blue 1. Bob has a red 2 on chop followed by another red 2. Alice could give Cathy a yellow Play Clue now, hoping Bob will then have nothing to do and discard so Alice can cleanly clue the red 2 afterward.

Should Alice give Cathy's yellow clue to force that discard?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No. Alice should play her blue 1.** Giving the unrelated yellow clue to steal a clue from Bob and force his discard is an Ego Clue. The better teamwork line is to empower Bob: Alice plays her known playable card and Bob can clue Cathy's yellow card himself. Accelerating the later red-2 clue is normally not worth forcing Bob into a discard from Alice's one-sided perspective.

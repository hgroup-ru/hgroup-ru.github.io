Again it is the Mid-Game with 8 clues. Alice clues red to Cathy's slot 3 red 2, apparently asking Bob to blind-play red 1. This time everybody can see that Donald's already-blue-clued card is the playable blue 2, so Alice had a legal higher-precedence Tempo Clue Stall available.

Does the 8 Clue Save interpretation still explain Alice's clue?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** The visible Tempo Clue Stall has precedence 3, while 8 Clue Save has precedence 4. Alice is not allowed to skip the available higher-precedence stall merely to make an 8 Clue Save. Therefore the team can rule out 8CS as the explanation for this clue and evaluate the clue under the ordinary conventions; if the Finesse is otherwise valid, Bob can react to it.

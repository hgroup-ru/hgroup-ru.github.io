<TabItem value="question">

It is End-Game. Red 3 is played, and Cathy has a blue 5 exactly one-away-from-chop. Alice clues number 5 to Cathy. Bob's Finesse Position card is the playable red 4.

Does the Level 21 Chop Move Ignition Interaction by itself force Bob to blind-play red 4?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** The special Chop Move Ignition Interaction for rank-5 clues is explicitly turned off in End-Game. Bob must therefore interpret the clue using the ordinary End-Game possibilities rather than automatically reacting as though every such rank-5 clue were a 5's Chop Move Ignition. The Mid-Game rule that turns off the Play Clue interpretation does not apply here.

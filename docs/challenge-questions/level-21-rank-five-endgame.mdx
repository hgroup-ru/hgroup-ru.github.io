<TabItem value="question">

Consider a rank-5 clue that focuses a 5 exactly one-away-from-chop. First, suppose it is Mid-Game and the 5 is actually playable. Second, suppose the same shape occurs in End-Game. Does the next player react the same way in both cases?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

No. During the active Level 21 interaction, even an **actually playable 5** is treated as though the rank-5 clue were not a Play Clue: the next player must react as if the 5 were not playable, because all such Play Clue interpretations are turned off. But the entire **Chop Move Ignition Interaction is turned off in End-Game**, so that special Level 21 rule no longer applies there.

</TabItem>

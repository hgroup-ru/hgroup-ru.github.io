<TabItem value="question">

Alice uses a Trash Finesse or Unknown Trash Discharge to make Bob blind-play a card that turns out to duplicate a globally known card. The clue did not pick up any extra useful cards. What extra message should the team read from this otherwise wasteful duplicate blind-play?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

An **Assisted Trash Chop Move** on the clue receiver. With no extra value to explain why the team intentionally caused the duplicate blind-play, the point of the clue is to Chop Move the cards to the right of the clued trash card(s). This is different from a Duplicitous Value Clue, where the extra touched value already justifies the duplication.

</TabItem>

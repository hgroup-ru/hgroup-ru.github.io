<TabItem value="question">

Blue 1 is played and Alice has a globally known yellow 1. Alice clues blue to Cathy, touching blue 1 on slot 2 as a Play Clue. Bob recognizes an Unknown Trash Discharge and blind-plays his Third Finesse Position, but that card is the duplicated yellow 1 and successfully plays. No additional cards were touched. Which cards should Cathy Chop Move?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Cathy should **Chop Move slots 3, 4, and 5**. The same Assisted Trash Chop Move logic works when the duplicate is reached through an Unknown Trash Discharge: with no extra value to explain the duplicate blind-play, the message is a Trash Chop Move on the clue receiver's cards to the right of the clued trash card.

Alice 5 Pulls Cathy's slot-4 green 2. Bob blind-plays red 1 from slot 1, but at clue time Cathy could see Bob also had a playable green 1 directly behind it. Must Cathy immediately play green 2 on her first turn after Bob's red 1?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Cathy must respect a possible **5 Pull Clandestine Finesse**. She can take an unrelated action and wait to see whether Bob later blind-plays the hidden green 1. If he does, that identifies Cathy's pulled card as green 2.

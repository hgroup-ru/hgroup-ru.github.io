<TabItem value="question">

A clue would otherwise set up a Certain Finesse, but it is being initiated from Bluff Seat, so the blind-playing player cannot know whether the clue might instead be a Bluff. May the team still rely on a Certain Discard if the initiator happens to hold the duplicate?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A Certain Discard is allowed only when the blind-playing player knows they have **not** been Bluffed. Therefore a Certain Finesse cannot be initiated from Bluff Seat. The safety of the discard comes from certainty that layering through the possible own-hand duplicate was illegal; once a Bluff interpretation is live, that certainty disappears.

</TabItem>

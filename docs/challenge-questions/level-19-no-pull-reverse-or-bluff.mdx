<TabItem value="question">

Alice initiates a 5 Pull whose pulled card is one-away-from-playable. Could she intend either (a) a Reverse Finesse that is not demonstrated before the pulled player's next turn, or (b) a Bluff where the pulled card does not connect to the blind-play?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Neither.** A 5 Pull Finesse must be demonstrated by a blind-play **between the clue and the pulled player's next turn**, so a pure Reverse Finesse is not allowed. And the pulled card always connects to the blind-play, so there is no 5 Pull Bluff. A Double Finesse may contain a later Reverse Finesse component, but the initial proof is still forward.

</TabItem>

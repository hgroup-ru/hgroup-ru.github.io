<TabItem value="question">

Alice clues Donald number 2 on an untouched 2. Bob blind-plays green 1, so Donald would now naturally identify his card as green 2. Cathy can see that the physical card is not green 2 and that her own Finesse Position is playable. Why must Cathy blind-play immediately?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

This can be a **Pestilent Double Bluff**. The first blind-play created a false synchronized identity for Donald's card. Cathy's immediate second blind-play is needed to break that false green-2 conclusion and resynchronize the team. After both blind-plays, Donald can eliminate both connecting identities associated with Bob and Cathy rather than treating Bob's play as an ordinary single Bluff/Finesse proof.

</TabItem>

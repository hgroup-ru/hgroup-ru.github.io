On the first turn, Alice clues number 2 to Donald's red 2. Bob blind-plays green 1. Cathy sees that if she does nothing, Donald will now believe his touched card is green 2 even though it is red 2. What should Cathy do?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Cathy should **immediately blind-play her Finesse Position** as a **Pestilent Double Bluff**. It reveals as blue 1 and successfully plays. Her second blind-play resynchronizes the team: Donald can rule out both green 2 and blue 2, because each identity would have made one of the two blind-plays unnecessary.

Nothing is played. Alice clues number 4 to Donald's red 4. Bob blind-plays red 1 and Cathy immediately blind-plays red 2. Does the matching red sequence promise that somebody must also hold and play red 3 for Donald?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** This is a **Hard Double Bluff**. The fact that exactly two blind-plays happened immediately after the clue tells the team not to extend the connection to red 3. Donald should treat his card as an unknown 4 rather than as a promised red 4 chain.

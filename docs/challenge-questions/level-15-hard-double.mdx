<TabItem value="question">

Nothing is played. Alice clues Donald number 4. Bob blind-plays red 1 and Cathy immediately blind-plays red 2. Donald's physical card is red 4. Is anyone now promised red 3 because the two blind-plays share the red suit with the 4?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Two immediate blind-plays identify a Hard Double Bluff. Even though red 1 and red 2 happen to share the suit of the physical red 4, the team does not invent a red-3 connector. Donald only knows the clued card can be a 4; the matching suits do not turn the Double Bluff into a three-card Finesse chain.

</TabItem>

<TabItem value="question">

All 1s are played and the team has no clues. Alice knows her red card is `r2`. Bob's slot 1 is the other `r2`, and his `g3` is on chop. Donald has `g2` in his Finesse Position.

How should Alice move?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice should **discard her known `r2` as a Gentleman's Discard**. The discard creates a clue, and Bob blind-plays his slot 1 as the transferred `r2`. Cathy can now spend the generated clue on Bob's chop `g3` as a Play Clue. Since `g3` is one-away-from-playable, Donald blind-plays his Finesse Position as `g2`; after that, Bob's clued `g3` is playable. The Gentleman's Discard costs Tempo, but here it buys the clue that turns Bob's endangered chop into a productive Finesse chain.

<TabItem value="question">

All 1s are played and the team has no clues. Alice knows her red card is `r2`. Bob's slot 1 is the other `r2`, while his `g3` is on chop. Cathy has a visible playable `g2`.

How should Alice move?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice should **discard her known `r2` as a Gentleman's Discard**. The discard creates a clue. Bob then blind-plays his slot 1 as `r2`; Cathy can play `g2`; and Alice gets another turn before Bob's next discard, so she can clue the now-playable `g3`. Here the side benefit is real and pays for the lost Tempo.

<TabItem value="question">

On turn 1, Alice clues Cathy number 3, touching a physical red 3. Bob blind-plays red 1. Does the shared red suit promise that Bob also has red 2?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** The clue was rank 3, and rank 1 does not connect to rank 3. This remains a 3 Bluff even though the physical cards share a suit. Cathy knows only that her card is a 3; Bob is not promised red 2 by this line.

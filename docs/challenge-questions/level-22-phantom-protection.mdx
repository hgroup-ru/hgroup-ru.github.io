<TabItem value="question">

A player has a Phantom Playable card approaching chop. The team can keep it by spending scarce clues and taking awkward emergency actions, but the position is otherwise tight. Does Save Principle require the team to preserve it at any cost?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

No. The team should **try** to protect Phantom Playable cards when practical, because they are nearly as important as Delayed Playable cards, and teammates will often discard to let the owner clue the in-between card. But Phantom Playable cards may be discarded when clues are scarce or the position is tight. Unlike playable and Delayed Playable cards, their protection is not absolute.

</TabItem>

<TabItem value="question">

Only one clue is available. Bob has a critical yellow 5 on chop because the other yellow 5 is already discarded. Cathy's red 4 on chop is Phantom Playable through Donald's visible, unclued red 2 and red 3.

Does Save Principle require Alice to spend the clue protecting Cathy's Phantom Playable instead of Bob's critical 5?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Alice must protect Bob's genuinely critical yellow 5. The team should try to preserve Phantom Playable cards when practical, but their protection is not absolute. In a tight position a Phantom Playable may be allowed to discard, especially when saving it would compete with a truly critical card.

<TabItem value="question">

A playable card is Chop Moved instead of being given the obvious direct Play Clue. Must the next player always react with an Ignition? Name the source-supported reasons that can make the Chop Move useful on its own. Also, what happens to Chop Move-related interpretations once there are no eligible cards left to Chop Move?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

No. A Chop Move Ignition exists only when there is **no other good reason** for the Chop Move. Level 21 specifically lists avoiding a Good Touch Principle violation, saving a Delayed Playable followed by a critical card, and obtaining an Elimination blind-play when the duplicate is visible. Once there are no eligible one-away-from-playable cards that cannot receive a direct Play or Save Clue, Chop Move-related clues are turned off and the action transforms into another interpretation, such as a Play Clue or some kind of Double Ignition.

</TabItem>

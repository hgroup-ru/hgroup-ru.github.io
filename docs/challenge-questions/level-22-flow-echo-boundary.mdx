<TabItem value="question">

Alice makes an emergency discard. Bob already has a known playable red 2. Cathy has no known play or safe discard, but her chop is an ordinary non-critical card: it is not a unique playable card, a critical card, or a unique 2.

Should Bob force this position into the standard Echo Scream or Generation branches of the Level 22 flowchart?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** This is the branch where the Level 22 flowchart deliberately stops. Alice has made an advanced move that the chart does not classify, or she may simply have made a mistake. Because Scream and Generation Discards must be last-resort actions, Bob has to reason from the actual cards that need saving and the relative safety of the chops instead of inventing a standard Echo or Generation label.

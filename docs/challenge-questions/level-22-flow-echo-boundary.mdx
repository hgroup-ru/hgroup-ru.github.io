<TabItem value="question">

Alice makes the flowchart's emergency discard and Bob already has a known play or known safe discard. Case A: Cathy has no known safe action but does have a unique playable, critical, or unique 2 on chop. Case B: Cathy has no known safe action and no such important chop. What does the flowchart say in each case?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

In case A, it is an **Echo Scream Discard Chop Move**: both Bob and Cathy Chop Move, and Bob does not need to clue Cathy's chop. In case B, the flowchart deliberately stops: Alice made **an advanced move not covered by this flowchart**, or Alice may have made a mistake. Level 22 says Scream and Generation Discards must be last-resort moves, so Bob must reason from which cards need saving and which chops are safest rather than forcing a standard label.

</TabItem>

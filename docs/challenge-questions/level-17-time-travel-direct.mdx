<TabItem value="question">

All 2s are played and Cathy has a globally known red 3. Alice clues number 3 to Bob, newly touching only the 3 on slot 2 as a Play Clue. Bob plays it expecting a non-red 3, but it is red 3 and successfully duplicates Cathy's known card. The clue gained no other card and provided no useful Tempo. What happens to Bob's cards behind slot 2?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob should **Chop Move the cards behind the duplicated red 3**. The clue has revealed itself as a genuine 0-for-1 with neither extra value nor Tempo, so it is a **Time Travel Chop Move (Direct Form)**. The team retroactively treats the duplicated card as if it had been directly clued for a Trash Chop Move on Bob's own hand.

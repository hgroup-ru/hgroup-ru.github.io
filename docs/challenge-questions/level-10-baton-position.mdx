<TabItem value="question">

Red 1 is played. Alice has a clued red card that she has fully identified as `r3`; it is not playable yet.

- **A:** Bob's matching `r3` is exactly on his Finesse Position.
- **B:** Bob has a playable `g1` on Finesse Position and the matching `r3` immediately behind it.

May Alice discard her known `r3` to transfer it to Bob in each position?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

- **A — yes.** Because `r3` is not playable, this is a Baton Discard. The matching card is exactly on Bob's Finesse Position.
- **B — no.** A Layered Baton Discard is explicitly forbidden. Unlike a Layered Gentleman's Discard, the Baton target must be exactly on the current Finesse Position.

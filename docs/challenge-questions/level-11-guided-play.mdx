<TabItem value="question">

Nothing is played. Alice gives Donald a red Play Clue on his red 2. Bob sees no red 1 in anyone else's hand, so Alice is clearly asking him to blind-play now. Cathy has a critical yellow 5 on chop.

Should Bob save Cathy's 5 instead of blind-playing?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No. Bob should blind-play.** Guide Principle applies when Bob is being directed into a Finesse or Bluff. Alice can see Cathy's endangered chop; if Bob needed to save it first, Alice should have delayed the clue. Bob therefore follows Alice's immediate guidance instead of independently overriding the line with a Save Clue.

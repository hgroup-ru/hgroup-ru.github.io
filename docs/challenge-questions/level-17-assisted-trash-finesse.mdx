<TabItem value="question">

Blue 1 is played and Alice has a globally known yellow 1. Alice clues number 1 to Cathy, touching blue 1 on slot 3 as a Play Clue. Bob reads a Trash Finesse and blind-plays his Finesse Position, but it is the duplicated yellow 1 and successfully plays. No additional cards were touched. What should Cathy do with slots 4 and 5?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Cathy should **Chop Move slots 4 and 5**. The otherwise wasteful duplicate blind-play, with no extra card value to explain it, reveals an **Assisted Trash Chop Move** on the clue receiver. The cards to the right of the clued trash card are the cards being moved.

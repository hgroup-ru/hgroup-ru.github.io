<TabItem value="question">

Alice makes a Blaze Discard. Cathy's matching card is on her **Second Finesse Position**, which happens to be slot 2. Bob has a positively clued card in slot 1, so Bob's Finesse Position is slot 2 and his Second Finesse Position is slot 3. Which slot should Bob blind-play to communicate the location?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Bob plays slot 3**, his own Second Finesse Position. Blaze Discard encodes a **Finesse Position ordinal**, not an absolute slot number. Bob matches Cathy's Second Finesse Position with his Second Finesse Position even though the physical slot numbers differ.

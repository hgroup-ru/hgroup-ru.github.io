<TabItem value="question">

Alice makes a non-emergency Blaze Discard of a known red 3. Bob sees the other red 3 in Cathy's completely unclued hand on slot 2, so it is Cathy's Second Finesse Position. Bob has a clued 5 on slot 1, which moves his own Finesse Position to slot 2 and his Second Finesse Position to slot 3.

Which slot must Bob blind-play to communicate the location of Cathy's red 3?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob must blind-play **slot 3**, his **Second Finesse Position**. Blaze Discards copy the matching card's **Finesse Position number**, not its raw slot number. Cathy's red 3 is on her Second Finesse Position, so Bob demonstrates his Second Finesse Position even though that happens to be slot 3 for him.

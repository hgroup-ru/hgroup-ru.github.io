<TabItem value="question">

A weird Gentleman's/Baton transfer is intended as Blaze Discard. Bob would prefer to take an unrelated action first and demonstrate the matching position on his next turn; additionally, a playable layer sits in front of the matching position. Is either delay or Layered Blaze allowed?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No to both.** Blaze Discard must be demonstrated by the **very next player immediately**, and Layered Blaze Discard is explicitly illegal on either the ignited player or the player holding the matching card. The convention is intentionally kept simple and position-explicit.

</TabItem>

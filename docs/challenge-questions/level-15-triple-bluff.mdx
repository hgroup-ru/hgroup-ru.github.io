<TabItem value="question">

After Alice's clue, Bob and Cathy have already blind-played unrelated playable cards as a Double Bluff. Donald also has a playable Finesse Position. Should Donald blind-play too to complete a Triple Bluff?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Triple Bluff is explicitly illegal. Bluff-style clues are defined to finish after at most two blind-plays in this progression. Donald must not extend the interpretation merely because another playable Finesse Position exists.

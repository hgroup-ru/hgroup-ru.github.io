<TabItem value="question">

Alice's clue has already caused Bob and Cathy to make the two immediate blind-plays of a Bluff-style line. Donald's clued red 5 is still not playable. Should Donald expect another player to supply a third blind-play connection before acting on the clue?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** H-Group explicitly has **no Triple Bluff**. Bluff-style clues are agreed to resolve after two blind-plays. If the clue still needs a third blind-play to make sense as a Bluff, that interpretation is not legal.

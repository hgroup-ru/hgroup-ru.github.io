<TabItem value="question">

Alice gives a valid 5 Pull to Cathy's slot-1 5. Cathy's slot 2 is already clued and already "gotten" by an earlier play clue; slot 3 is an untouched playable green 1. Which card does this new 5 Pull get?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Slot 3.** A 5 Pull skips over a card immediately to the right of the 5 when that card is already clued or already gotten, and pulls the next card instead. It does not redundantly get slot 2 again.

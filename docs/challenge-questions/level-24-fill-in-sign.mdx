<TabItem value="question">

In both cases Alice uses the same complicated move to get a card that could instead receive a direct Play Clue. In case A, her clue positively touches a previously-clued card and fills in new positive information. In case B, the only extra information on the previously-clued card is a new negative clue.

Which case can use the fill-in as a side benefit that prevents the move from being Unnecessary?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Only **case A**. Positive fill-in counts as a side benefit and can make the complicated move Necessary. For simplicity, **negative fill-in does not count**. Therefore case B remains Unnecessary if there is no other side benefit.

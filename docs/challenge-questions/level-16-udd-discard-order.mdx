<TabItem value="question">

Cathy has just recognized an Unknown Dupe Discharge. Its focused duplicate is not the leftmost known-trash card in her hand. Which card is she expected to discard first?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

She normally discards the **UDD focus first**, even before older known trash to its left. This is the Level 16 exception to the earlier left-to-right Known-Trash Discard Order. The focus-first convention prevents desynchronization about whether Cathy recognized a UDD/UTD and which card is actually safe to discard.

</TabItem>

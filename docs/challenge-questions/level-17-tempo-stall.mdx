<TabItem value="question">

The same kind of duplicate play reveals a Duplicitous Tempo Clue, but this time Alice is in a stalling situation where an ordinary Tempo Clue Stall is legal at the relevant precedence. Does the fact that the played card was a duplicate automatically add a Tempo Clue Chop Move?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Duplication does not create a new Chop Move rule. A Duplicitous Tempo Clue may or may not Chop Move under exactly the same conditions as an ordinary Tempo Clue. If the clue is legitimately explained as a Tempo Clue Stall, there is no extra Chop Move merely because a duplicate was involved.

<TabItem value="question">

Red 1, green 1, and blue 1 are played, and Cathy has a globally known red 2. Alice is in a Stalling Situation where a Tempo Clue Stall is allowed at the current precedence. She clues number 2 to Bob, filling his already-touched green card in as green 2 and focusing another 2. Bob plays the focus expecting blue 2, but it is red 2 and duplicates Cathy's known card.

Should Bob also Chop Move his chop because the focused play was a duplicate?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Duplication does not create a new Chop Move rule. A Duplicitous Tempo Clue may or may not Chop Move under exactly the same conditions as an ordinary Tempo Clue. Here the clue is legitimately explained as a Tempo Clue Stall, so there is no extra Chop Move merely because a duplicate was involved.

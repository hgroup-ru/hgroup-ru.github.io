<TabItem value="question">

It is the Mid-Game with 8 clues. Alice has no higher-precedence stall available. She wants to clue red to Cathy's slot 3 red 2 and expects Bob to blind-play his Finesse Position as red 1.

Can Alice rely on Bob reading this as a Finesse?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** At 8 clues, a clue that saves an arbitrary card outside slot 1 can be an **8 Clue Save** when no higher-precedence clue is available. Bob therefore cannot safely treat a tricky-looking clue like this as a Finesse; the stalling interpretation is live. Alice must choose a line whose intended interpretation survives the 8-clue context.

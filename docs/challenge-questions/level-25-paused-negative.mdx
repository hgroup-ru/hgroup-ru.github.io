<TabItem value="question">

Bob is Finessed for red 1 and red 2 in what he knows is a Layered Finesse. This time his first blind-play is actually red 1, the next connecting card of the original Finesse. Bob also has a fully-known playable card that would otherwise create a Priority Finesse.

May Bob pause now and play that other card before red 2?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Playing the actual red 1 connector has **not** demonstrated to the team that Bob is still Finessed for red 2. The special Paused Priority Finesse permission applies only after an unrelated blind-play proves that a layer remains. Bob must keep resolving the original Finesse instead of sending a new Priority signal.

</TabItem>

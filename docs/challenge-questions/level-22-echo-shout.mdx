<TabItem value="question">

Alice has a known playable blue 2, but instead she discards a different card that is globally known trash. Bob, the next player, already has a known playable red 2. Someone proposes that the discard should therefore bounce through Bob as an Echo Shout.

Can it?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Shout Discards never create Echo Chop Moves. Discarding known trash is not severe enough to carry the extra bounce meaning of an unknown-card Scream Discard. Alice's action must be interpreted as either a **single Shout Discard Chop Move** or a **Generation Discard**, depending on the rest of the position; Bob's safe action does not turn it into an Echo Shout.

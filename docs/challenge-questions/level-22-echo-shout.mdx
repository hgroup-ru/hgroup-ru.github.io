<TabItem value="question">

Alice discards known trash instead of playing, and someone proposes that the resulting Shout Discard should bounce through multiple players as an Echo Shout. Is that interpretation allowed? What are the two possible interpretations instead?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

No. **Shout Discards cannot create Echo Chop Moves.** Discarding known trash is much less severe than intentionally discarding an unknown card, so it cannot carry the extra Echo meaning. The action is always either a **single Shout Discard Chop Move** or a **Generation Discard**.

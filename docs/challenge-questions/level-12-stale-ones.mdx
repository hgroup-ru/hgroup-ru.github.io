<TabItem value="question">

After several ordinary clues, Alice made the first completely unknown discard of the game, ending the Early Game. Bob then immediately gave Cathy a number 1 clue touching two previously untouched 1s. If both 1s were good, Alice would have been required to clue them before discarding.

Which of the two cards should Cathy play first, and what should she conclude about the other one?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Cathy should **skip the oldest/rightmost 1 and play the newer/leftmost 1**. This is a Stale 1's Clue. Alice's unknown discard implied that no ordinary Play Clue or Save Clue remained, so both newly clued 1s cannot be good. By convention the oldest 1 is the stale one Cathy would otherwise have played first, so she marks it known-trash and plays the newer 1.

<TabItem value="question">

Alice performs an Unnecessary move using known trash, so the extra effect is an Ignition. Bob is the next player, but he already has a known playable card. Bob can also see playable Finesse Position cards on Cathy and Donald, with Donald later in turn order.

Who receives the Ignition?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Donald.** Known-trash Unnecessary moves normally Ignite Bob, but this is one of the explicit exceptions: when Bob already has a play and can see playable Finesse Position cards later in turn order, the Ignition goes to the **last player with a visible playable Finesse Position**. Here that player is Donald.

</TabItem>

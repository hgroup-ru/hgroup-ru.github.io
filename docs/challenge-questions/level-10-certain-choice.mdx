Red is stacked through 2. Cathy has a red-clued card with negative 4 information, so from her perspective it is `r3` or `r5`. Cathy gives Bob a red Play Clue on `r4` as a Reverse Finesse. Bob then takes an unrelated safe discard. It is now Alice's turn, and Alice knows that her Finesse Position is promised as `r3`.

Compare the two visible positions shown below. In **A**, Cathy's ambiguous red card is actually `r3`; in **B**, it is actually `r5`. What should Alice do with her own Finesse Position?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

- **A:** Alice should Certain Discard her Finesse Position. She can see the `r3` in Cathy's hand. A Layered Finesse through a card that could already be clued in the initiator's hand is forbidden, so Alice can be certain that her blind card is the duplicate `r3` and safely pass it back.
- **B:** Alice should blind-play her Finesse Position as `r3`. The visible clued card in Cathy's hand is `r5`, so there is no duplicated `r3` that would justify a Certain Discard.

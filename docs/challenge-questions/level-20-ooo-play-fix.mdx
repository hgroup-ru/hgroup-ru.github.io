<TabItem value="question">

On the first turn Cathy holds, newest to oldest, red 4, red 1, red 2, red 3, blue 4. Alice clues red, so the clue initially focuses the red 4 as though it were red 1. What should Bob do, and after that correction should Cathy abandon Alice's original focus?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob gives the required **Fix Clue**, here number 4. Cathy then uses the earlier red cards in order and **still goes on to play the original red-4 focus**. In an Out-of-Order Play Clue, the Fix corrects the immediate identity error; it does not cancel the original promise that the focus is playable. The blue 4 touched by the Fix is collateral damage and is not promised playable.

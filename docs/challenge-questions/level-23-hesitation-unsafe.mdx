<TabItem value="question">

Bob receives a Play Clue and then hesitates by discarding in a safe, clue-rich position. Cathy sees that Bob could be waiting either for a connector already clued in Cathy's hand **or** for Cathy's Finesse Position. May Cathy blind-play her Finesse Position as a Hesitation Blind-Play?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Hesitation Blind-Play is only safe when the hesitation uniquely points to that blind card. If Bob could be waiting for a clued card or the Finesse Position, Cathy cannot know which connector Alice intended. Acting on the guess risks a misplay. Similar safety blocks apply to Self-Finesse hesitation, an identical copy of the clued card, and plays that would make the clue look like a Double Finesse with a self component.

</TabItem>

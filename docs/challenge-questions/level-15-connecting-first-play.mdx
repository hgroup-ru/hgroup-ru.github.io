<TabItem value="question">

Red 1 is played. Alice clues red to Donald's red 4. Bob's Finesse Position turns out to be red 2, so his blind-play directly connects toward the clued red card. Does that connection let Cathy stop instead of making the second immediate blind-play from Finesse Position?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A Double Bluff remains valid even when Bob's first blind-play connects to the clued card. Cathy may not know whether the clue is a Double Bluff or a Double Finesse until after she blind-plays, but she still has to make that second immediate blind-play.

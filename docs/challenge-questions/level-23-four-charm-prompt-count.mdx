<TabItem value="question">

Alice gives a Play Clue to a previously untouched 4 that is not yet playable. Bob can see a valid connection in which one already-clued card is Prompted and then Bob must make two blind-plays before the 4 becomes playable.

Does the Prompt count together with the two blind-plays as the "three" that should make Bob abandon the Finesse for a 4 Charm?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** For the 4 Charm threshold, players count only **blind-plays**. Prompts do not count toward the "three or more blind-plays" test. A connection containing one Prompt plus two blind-plays is therefore still read as the ordinary Prompt/Finesse line, not as a 4 Charm for that reason alone.

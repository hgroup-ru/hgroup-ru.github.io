<TabItem value="question">

All 1s are played. Cathy's hand from newest to oldest is green 4, blue 1, red 2, yellow 4, green 4. Alice clues number 1 to slot 2. The resulting Chop Move Ignition spans slots 3 through 5: slot 3 is playable red 2, and slot 5 is a copy of Cathy's other unclued green 4.

Which of slots 3, 4, and 5 are actually Chop Moved, and what may Cathy infer about slot 4?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Only the **leftmost and rightmost boundary cards**, slots 3 and 5, are Chop Moved. Slot 4 is a middle card and is **not** Chop Moved at all. Cathy gets no identity promise about it from the Chop Move Ignition; it may be any card. The multi-card conditions constrain the playable left boundary and the trash-or-duplicate right boundary, not cards in between.

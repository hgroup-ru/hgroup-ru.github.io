<TabItem value="question">

A Chop Move Ignition spans three or more cards between the touched clue and chop. What does the convention promise about the cards strictly between the leftmost and rightmost relevant cards? Should the clue-receiver mark those middle cards as Chop Moved too?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Nothing is promised about those **middle cards**. If three or more cards are in the span, the middle cards are explicitly **not Chop Moved** and may have any identity. Only the boundary conditions for the leftmost and rightmost Chop Moved cards carry the Level 21 promise.

</TabItem>

<TabItem value="question">

It is Early Game and Low Score Phase. Alice gives a number-5 clue to a 5 that is exactly one-away from chop, even though a normal Play Clue or Save Clue was available, so this cannot be a legal 5 Stall. What does the clue do at Level 19?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

It is an **Early 5's Chop Move**. Because a 5 Stall was not allowed and the touched 5 is exactly one-away from chop, the special message is to Chop Move the card to the right of the 5. Do not treat the rank-5 clue as a direct Play Clue in the Low Score Phase.

</TabItem>

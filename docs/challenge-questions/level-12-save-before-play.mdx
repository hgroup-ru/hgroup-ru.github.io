It is the first turn. Cathy's hand, newest to oldest, is blue 4, blue 4, red 3, red 1, blue 5. No one else has red 1. Alice wants red 1 played and knows blue 5 must eventually be saved.

Should Alice clue the red 1 immediately, or save the blue 5 first?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice should **give the 5 Save first**. Cluing the red 1 immediately and then saving blue 5 gives only 2-for-2.

**After Alice's 5 Save:**

<AfterAlice />

Now the saved 5 is no longer chop, so Bob can follow with a red Chop-Focus Play Clue that gets red 1 and red 3 as a 2-for-1.

**After Bob's red clue:**

<AfterBob />

Together the two clues obtain three useful cards for **3-for-2**.

<TabItem value="question">

A duplicitous clue causes a duplicate to play but introduces no other valuable new card. It does, however, fill in an already-touched card and reveal that card as playable. What should the team retroactively call the clue?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

A **Duplicitous Tempo Clue**. With no extra valuable pickup, the useful remaining effect is the tempo/fill-in on the already-touched playable card. It may additionally cause a Tempo Clue Chop Move under the same conditions as an ordinary Tempo Clue; the duplication does not create a new special criterion for that Chop Move.

</TabItem>

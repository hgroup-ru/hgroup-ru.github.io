<TabItem value="question">

At 8 clues, Alice can see that Bob's blue-clued card is the playable blue 2, so Alice knows she had a higher-precedence Tempo Clue Stall available. Instead she clues red to Cathy's slot 3 red 2, intending Bob's slot 1 to Finesse as red 1. Bob, however, only knows that his blue-clued card is blue; he does not know it is the playable blue 2.

Can Alice rely on Bob using the skipped Tempo Stall to rule out 8 Clue Save?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** The precedence argument only helps a reacting player when that player can know the higher-precedence option existed. From Bob's information, Alice might have had no Tempo Clue Stall at all, so an 8 Clue Save remains a possible explanation. This makes the intended Finesse unsafe and desynchronization-prone even though Alice and the other players can see why it was not meant as a stall.

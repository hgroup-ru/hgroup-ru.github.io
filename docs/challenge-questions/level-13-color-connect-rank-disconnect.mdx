<TabItem value="question">

Nothing is played in a four-player game. Alice gives Donald a red Play Clue touching one card. Donald already has negative 2 information on that card. Bob blind-plays red 1. Cathy now has to decide whether Alice intended a 3 Bluff or a Double Finesse.

Should Cathy blind-play her Finesse Position as red 2?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Yes.** Donald's negative 2 information prevents Donald from later mistaking his card for red 2, but it does not turn the line into a 3 Bluff. The clue is red and Bob's blind-play is red, so the clue **connects** to the blind-play. Level 13 therefore treats the line as a Double Finesse, and Cathy is promised red 2 on Finesse Position.

</TabItem>

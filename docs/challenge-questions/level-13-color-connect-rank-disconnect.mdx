<TabItem value="question">

Nothing is played in a four-player game. Alice clues Donald red. Donald's touched card has negative 2 information. Bob blind-plays red 1. Should Cathy treat the line as a 3 Bluff and decline to blind-play?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** The negative 2 information prevents Donald from later mistaking his own card for red 2, but it does not disconnect the clue from Bob's blind-play. **Red connects to red**, so Cathy must treat the line as a Double Finesse and blind-play her Finesse Position as red 2.

<TabItem value="question">

An Unnecessary advanced move uses a clue target that is **unknown trash and off chop**. After the move's ordinary blind-play/discharge effect resolves, what additional hand effect is communicated to the clue receiver?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

A **Chop Move**, as though a Trash Chop Move had been given. This can create information asymmetry: for many unnecessary advanced moves, the player who blind-played will not initially know the clue receiver's card was Chop Moved while the other players can see it. The team must preserve that desynchronization until the Chop Move is demonstrated.

</TabItem>

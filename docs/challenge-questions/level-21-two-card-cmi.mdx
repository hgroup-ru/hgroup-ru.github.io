<TabItem value="question">

Alice makes a Chop Move that would move two cards and wants it to function as a Chop Move Ignition. What must be true about the two moved edge cards for this multi-card CMI to be legal?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

The **leftmost Chop Moved card must be playable**, and the **rightmost Chop Moved card must be trash or a copy of an unclued card in the same hand**. If three or more cards are crossed by the move, nothing is promised about the middle cards; they are not themselves the special edge commitments.

</TabItem>

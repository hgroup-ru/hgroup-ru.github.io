<TabItem value="question">

Alice has two red-2 Elimination candidates, slots 4 and 5, and the true red 2 is actually on chop slot 5. Slot 4 is also independently playable. May the team intentionally give no clue and let Alice perform the normal Riding Deduction first?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Yes.** This is a **Riding Deduction Bluff**. Alice first plays the unrelated playable slot 4 because the Ride tells her the Elimination card is not on chop. After that play, only slot 5 retains the red-2 possibility, so Alice can play the true red 2 on her next turn.

<TabItem value="question">

Alice plays a non-Priority known card that would normally suggest the next connector. Bob can see the actual connector in Cathy's hand, but it is **not** on Cathy's Finesse Position. Should Cathy still blind-play her Finesse Position to honor Priority?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No. Priority Finesses are turned off.** When the promised connector is visible in someone else's non-Finesse Position, the team is committed to giving that card a direct **Load Clue**. The receiver interprets the Load Clue as a fresh normal Play Clue, not as a Fix Clue, and rewinds/deletes the Priority-related notes before reinterpreting it. A clue remains a Load Clue even if its surface shape would otherwise look like a Save Clue.

</TabItem>

<TabItem value="question">

All 1s are played. Alice clues red to Cathy's red 1 as if it were a Play Clue. From Cathy's perspective, a normal Bluff blind-play would make the card look like useful, currently unplayable red 3 instead of proving trash. What reaction should Bob use?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob should blind-play his **Third Finesse Position**, signaling an **Unknown Trash Discharge**. A normal Trash Bluff is only sufficient when the touched card's possibilities are playable, delayed playable, or trash. Here a normal Bluff would leave a useful unplayable possibility and would lie to Cathy.

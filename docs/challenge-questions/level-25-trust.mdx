<TabItem value="question">

Alice can play a fully known red 2 that leads directly into Bob's clued red 3. Instead she plays a globally unknown playable 2. Under what condition should Bob nevertheless treat this as a Priority-style signal?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Only when choosing the unknown card is **extremely suboptimal** in context, strong enough that ordinary information-gathering cannot explain it. Then the exceptional interpretation is a **Trust Finesse** (or analogous Trust Prompt/Bluff). The threshold is intentionally much stronger than ordinary Priority because unknown plays normally do not signal.

</TabItem>

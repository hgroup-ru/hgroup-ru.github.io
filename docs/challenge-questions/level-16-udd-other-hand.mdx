<TabItem value="question">

Alice gives Cathy a clue that unexpectedly duplicates a card already touched in **Donald's** hand. There is no ordinary reason for the duplication. Can Bob conclude that this is an Unknown Dupe Discharge and play his Third Finesse Position?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Unknown Dupe Discharge only applies when the duplicated cards are in the **same recipient's hand**. Duplicating a card in somebody else's hand is explicitly outside the convention, so Bob must search for another interpretation instead of manufacturing a UDD.

</TabItem>

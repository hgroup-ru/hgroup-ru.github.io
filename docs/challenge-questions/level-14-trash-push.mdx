<TabItem value="question">

All 1s are already played. Alice clues number 1 to Bob, touching only his unclued chop card on slot 5. What should Bob do with the new chop card on slot 4?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob should **blind-play slot 4 immediately**. Cluing known trash on chop cannot perform a normal Trash Chop Move because the touched card was already chop. Spending a clue there therefore means **Trash Push**: the new chop is pushed onto the table and should generally be demonstrated immediately.

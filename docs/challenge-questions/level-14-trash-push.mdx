<TabItem value="question">

All 1s are already played. Alice clues number 1 to Bob and the clue only touches Bob's chop card, which is a known-trash 1. What should Bob do with the card immediately to its left?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob should **blind-play the card immediately to the left of chop**. Cluing known trash on chop cannot be a useful Trash Chop Move, because it would move nothing. The deliberate useless clue is therefore a Trash Push, which pushes Bob's new chop card onto the table. The pushed card must be immediately playable and is normally demonstrated immediately.

<TabItem value="question">

Alice's clue can be an ordinary Bluff, so Bob may be the reacting blind-player. Before resolving it, Bob notices another tempting Bluff he could initiate with his own clue. Is starting that second Bluff the normal expert line?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Deferring a possible Bluff to perform **another Bluff should almost never be done**. It fractures the game into competing hypothetical states for different clue recipients. A possible Double Bluff makes the prohibition even stronger: then Bob must not defer at all.

</TabItem>

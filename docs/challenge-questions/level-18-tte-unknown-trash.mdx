<TabItem value="question">

Red 4, green 1, and blue 1 are played. Alice clues Bob red, touching his red 4 on slot 3. Bob can see Cathy's red 5, so he knows his red card is trash and discards it for a Trash Chop Move. Cathy did not know Bob's card was trash and is surprised by the discard. Red 5 is the only remaining useful red identity. What does Cathy infer?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Cathy infers that **red 5 is in her own hand** and writes red-5 Elimination Notes. Bob could only know his red card was trash by seeing the last useful red card elsewhere. If two or more good red identities or copies were still possible, this proof would be absent and the expected interpretation would instead be a Trash Finesse.

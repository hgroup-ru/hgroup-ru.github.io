<TabItem value="question">

Alice gives a Play Clue to a previously untouched, currently unplayable 4. Prompt is impossible, and Bob would need to blind-play **three** cards from his own hand to satisfy the natural Finesse. Cathy does not provide a plausible 4 Double Bluff line. Which fallback should Bob consider?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

A **4 Charm**: Bob blind-plays his **Fourth Finesse Position**. One or two Bob blind-plays still favor the Finesse interpretation; three makes the Finesse unlikely enough to reach Charm after the earlier interpretations are excluded. Prompts do not count toward the three-blind-play threshold.

</TabItem>

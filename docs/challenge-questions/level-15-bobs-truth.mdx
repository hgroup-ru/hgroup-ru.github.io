<TabItem value="question">

Bob can see that Alice's clue has exactly two plausible explanations. One is a Double Bluff with fewer blind-plays; the other is a truthful Finesse that requires more blind-plays. Bob is the first reacting player.

Should Occam's Razor make Bob choose the shorter Double Bluff line?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** When Bob's choice is specifically between a Double Bluff and a truthful Finesse, **Bob's Truth Principle overrides Occam's Razor**. Bob assumes the Finesse even when it needs more blind-plays. The simpler-looking Double Bluff does not win merely by being shorter.

</TabItem>

On the first turn, Alice clues number 4 to Bob's slot 1. Bob sees a playable red 1 on Cathy's Finesse Position. The clue could be a Reverse Finesse on red 4 that eventually needs red 1, red 2, and red 3, or a shorter Double Bluff. Which interpretation should Bob choose?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob should assume the **Reverse Finesse**. Normally Occam's Razor prefers the interpretation with fewer blind-plays and Prompts, but here **Bob's Truth Principle takes precedence**: when Bob can see a choice between a Double Bluff and a Finesse, he assumes the Finesse even when it is longer.

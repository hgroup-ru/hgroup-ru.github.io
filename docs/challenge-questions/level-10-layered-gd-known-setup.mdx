<TabItem value="question">

Red 1 is played. Alice knows her red card is `r3`. Bob has a known `r2` ready to play. Cathy has `g1` in slot 1 and the other `r3` in slot 2.

Alice considers discarding `r3`, expecting Bob to play `r2` before Cathy acts. Can that discard be a valid special transfer?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

No. At the moment Alice discards, `r3` is still unplayable, so the discard cannot be a Gentleman's Discard; a Gentleman's Discard only transfers a card that is already playable. An exact unplayable card would instead be a Baton Discard, but Cathy's matching `r3` is in slot 2 behind `g1`, not exactly on her Finesse Position. That would be an illegal Layered Baton Discard. Bob's future `r2` play does not retroactively turn the discard into a legal GD.

</TabItem>

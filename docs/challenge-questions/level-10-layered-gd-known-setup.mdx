<TabItem value="question">

Red 1 is played. Alice knows her red card is `r3`. Bob has a known `r2` ready to play. Cathy has `g1` in slot 1 and the other `r3` in slot 2.

What should Alice do?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice can **discard `r3` as a Layered Gentleman's Discard**. Bob's already-known `r2` advances red before Cathy needs the transferred card. Cathy's `g1` is the playable layer in front of the `r3`; after that layer is removed, the promised `r3` remains behind it.

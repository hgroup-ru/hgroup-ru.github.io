<TabItem value="question">

Alice wants to use a Blaze Discard, but on Bob's side the Finesse Position that would have to demonstrate the matching card is hidden behind an unrelated playable layer. Bob could theoretically blind-play through that layer and eventually reach the required position.

Is Alice allowed to use that layered sequence as the Blaze demonstration?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A **Layered Blaze Discard is illegal**, whether the layer is on the ignited player or on the player holding the matching card. Blaze is intentionally kept simple: the required Finesse Position must be demonstrated directly rather than recovered through a layered sequence.

</TabItem>

<TabItem value="question">

All 1s are played. Alice clues red to Cathy, newly touching a focused red 1 on slot 2 and a red 5 on slot 3. Bob signals an Unknown Trash Discharge. Which touched cards may Cathy mark as trash?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Only the **focused slot 2** is trash. In a multi-touch Unknown Trash Discharge, Good Touch Principle still applies to the non-focused cards, so Cathy's red 5 remains useful. This is deliberately the opposite of a multi-touch Trash Bluff, where all touched cards are treated as trash.

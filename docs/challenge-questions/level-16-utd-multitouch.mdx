All 1s are played. Alice clues red to Cathy, newly touching a focused card on slot 2 and another card on slot 3. Bob blind-plays his Third Finesse Position and it successfully plays, signaling an Unknown Trash Discharge. Which touched cards may Cathy mark as trash?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Only the **focused slot 2** is trash. In a multi-touch Unknown Trash Discharge, Good Touch Principle still applies to non-focused cards, so Cathy knows the red card on slot 3 remains useful but does not learn its exact rank from the Discharge. This is deliberately the opposite of a multi-touch Trash Bluff, where all touched cards are treated as trash.

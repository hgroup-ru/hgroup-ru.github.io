<TabItem value="question">

An Unknown Trash Discharge clue newly touches two cards. After the Discharge is demonstrated, should the receiver mark **both** touched cards as trash, as they would after a multi-touch Trash Bluff?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No. Only the focus is trash.** In an Unknown Trash Discharge, Good Touch Principle still applies to the non-focused newly touched cards, so they are expected to be useful. This is deliberately the opposite of a Trash Bluff, where all newly touched cards are treated as trash.

</TabItem>

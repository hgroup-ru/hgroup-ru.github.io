<TabItem value="question">

On turn 1, Alice clues Cathy red on a physical red 3. Bob blind-plays red 1. Is this a legal 3 Bluff?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Red connects to red. Cathy therefore reads Bob's red 1 as a Finesse connection and expects her touched red card to be red 2. With no extra information to stop her, she would misplay the red 3 as red 2. A Bluff is illegal when it looks like this connecting Finesse.

</TabItem>

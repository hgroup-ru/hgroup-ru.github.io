<TabItem value="question">

Alice clued Cathy red on the first turn. Bob then blind-played red 1. The touched card is physically red 3. Is this a legal way to perform a 3 Bluff?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Red connects to red. Cathy therefore reads Bob's red 1 as the first card of a Finesse and expects the touched red card to be red 2. With no extra information stopping her, she would play the red 3 as red 2 and misplay. A 3 Bluff must not masquerade as this connecting Finesse line.

</TabItem>

All 1s are played and the team is out of clues. Alice knows her red card is `r2`. Bob has a playable `g2` in slot 1, the other `r2` immediately behind it, and an untouched `y5` on chop.

How should Alice move?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice should **Gentleman's Discard the `r2`**. This is a Layered Gentleman's Discard: Bob first blind-plays the playable `g2` layer, while the transferred `r2` remains promised behind it. The discard also creates a clue; Cathy can use that clue to 5 Save Bob's untouched `y5` before Bob next has to discard. Here the side benefit is concrete, so the lost Tempo is compensated.

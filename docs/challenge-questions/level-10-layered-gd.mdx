All 1s are played and the team is out of clues. Alice knows her red card is `r2`. Bob has a playable `g2` in slot 1 and the other `r2` immediately behind it.

How should Alice move?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice can **Gentleman's Discard the `r2`**. This is a Layered Gentleman's Discard: Bob's first card is a playable layer, and the transferred `r2` is behind it. The safe discard also generates a clue token, so there is a concrete reason to choose the GD instead of simply playing `r2` now.

</TabItem>

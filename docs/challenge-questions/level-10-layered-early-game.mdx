<TabItem value="question">

Earlier, Bob discarded a known `r1` as a Gentleman's Discard to Alice. Alice blind-played her Finesse Position expecting `r1`, but successfully played `g1` instead. The promised `r1` therefore moved to her next slot.

No completely unknown card has been discarded yet, so Early Game is still active. The team has not yet made its required 5 Stall, and Cathy has an eligible off-chop `y5`.

It is Alice's turn again. Should she give the 5 Stall now, or act on the surviving GD promise?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice should blind-play the promised next slot as `r1`.

The special GD discard did not end Early Game, and the successful `g1` did not erase the promise: this is a Layered Gentleman's Discard. However, a 5 Stall is a last-resort action. Alice currently has an immediate promised play, so she should resolve it first. The team can still make the required 5 Stall later, before the first completely unknown discard, if it remains available.

</TabItem>

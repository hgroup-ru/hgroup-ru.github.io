<TabItem value="question">

Alice just misplayed one copy of red 4. Bob cannot see the other red 4 anywhere, and his completely unknown chop card could therefore be the last copy. Discarding it would lower the maximum score. Bob has no normal Play Clue or Save Clue.

May Bob simply discard his chop?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A Double Discard Situation can begin after the previous player **discards or misplays** a card. Because Bob's discard could throw away the last copy of that same red 4 and lower the maximum score, Double Discard Avoidance applies. Bob must not discard; he must give a clue, using an allowed stall clue if no normal Play Clue or Save Clue exists.

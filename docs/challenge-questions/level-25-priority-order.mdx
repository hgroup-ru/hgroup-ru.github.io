<TabItem value="question">

Alice has several fully known playable cards. One is a required blind-play, one leads into a clued teammate card, one leads into her own hand, and another is a 5. Which category has the highest Priority?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

The **blind-play**. Level 25 Priority starts with required blind-plays, then cards that lead into clued/Finessed cards in someone else's hand, then cards leading into the player's own hand, then 5s, then lower rank, then leftmost. Playing a lower-priority fully known card can deliberately signal a Priority move.

</TabItem>

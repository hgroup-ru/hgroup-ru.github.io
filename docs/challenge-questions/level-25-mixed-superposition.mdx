<TabItem value="question">

Alice can play a fully-known red 3. She also has an unknown playable card whose remaining possibilities are blue 2 and another 3 that would have lower Priority than the red 3 because of its position. Thus red 3 has lower Priority than one possible identity of the unknown card but higher Priority than the other. Alice plays red 3.

Must the team read a Priority signal from that choice?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A fully-known card played over an unknown card can trigger Priority only when the known card has lower Priority than **every possible identity** of the unknown card. Here the superposition is mixed, so Alice's red 3 is not unambiguously the lower-Priority choice. No special Priority signal is forced.

</TabItem>

<TabItem value="question">

On the first turn Alice clues Bob red. Bob's focused slot 1 is really red 3, slot 2 is red 2, and Cathy has red 1 in Finesse Position. Bob respects a possible Reverse Finesse and discards; Cathy blind-plays red 1. What must happen before Bob can safely continue?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice must give Bob a **Fix Clue**, here number 2, before Bob would misplay red 3 as red 2. Bob plays the corrected red 2 and then knows the original focused red card must be red 3, because that focus was originally clued as playable and caused the Reverse Finesse. Unlike an ordinary post-Fix stop, the Out-of-Order line continues through the original focus.

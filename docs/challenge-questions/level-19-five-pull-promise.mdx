<TabItem value="question">

Donald has clued red 1 and blue 1. Cathy was 5 Pulled and her pulled card could therefore be red 2 or blue 2 as a Delayed Play Clue. Before either 1 plays, Bob gives Donald a Play Clue on the currently unplayable red 3. What does that tell Cathy?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Cathy is now **promised red 2** as her pulled card. A follow-up Play Clue to a currently unplayable card lets the 5-Pulled player discard the broad Delayed Play interpretation: the pulled card is promised to be the connector that makes that new Play Clue work.

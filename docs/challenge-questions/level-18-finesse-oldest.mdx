<TabItem value="question">

Blue 2 is played. Alice has blue-3 Elimination Notes on slots 2, 3, 4, and 5. Bob clues Cathy blue 4 and Cathy discards, so Alice is being Reverse Finessed for blue 3. Slot 5 is already Chop Moved. Which card should Alice play?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice plays the **oldest non-Chop-Moved Elimination candidate: slot 4**. Elimination Finesse does not use ordinary Finesse Position; it promises the oldest card carrying the relevant Elimination Note, while skipping Chop Moved candidates. If all candidates were Chop Moved, she would use the oldest of those instead.

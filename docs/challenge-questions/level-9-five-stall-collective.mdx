<TabItem value="question">

It is still the Early Game. A few turns ago, Alice gave Bob an off-chop 5 clue purely as a stall. Now all normal Play Clues and Save Clues have been exhausted. Cathy has another untouched off-chop 5, and Donald is about to act with nothing else that the Early Game requires.

Does Donald have to clue Cathy's 5 before the team may make its first unknown discard?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** The Early Game requires the team to perform **one** 5 Stall if possible, not one for every available off-chop 5. Alice's earlier stall already satisfied the collective obligation. Donald may give another 5 Stall, but Cathy's 5 does not prevent the team from entering the Mid-Game.

It is the Early Game. All required normal Play Clues and Save Clues have been extinguished. Bob's already-clued yellow 5 received the team's first valid 5 Stall earlier. Cathy still has another unclued off-chop green 5. No other rule forces a clue now.

Must the team give the second 5 Stall before someone may make the first unknown discard?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** In the Early Game, the team must collectively perform **at least one** 5 Stall if possible. Once that obligation has been satisfied, additional available 5 Stalls are optional. Players may give more of them before entering the Mid-Game, but they are not required to do so.

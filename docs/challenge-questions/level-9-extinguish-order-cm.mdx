<TabItem value="question">

It is the Early Game and Bob acts next. He already has two 1s clued in his hand, and an otherwise-untouched red 4 is on his chop. The only remaining visible Save Clue would protect that red 4. No other mandatory Early Game action remains.

Must Alice save the red 4 before the team can make its first completely unknown discard?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** With two 1s already clued, Bob is expected to Order Chop Move the card that would otherwise sit on chop. Level 9 therefore does not require Alice to spend the last Early Game clue saving a card that the upcoming Order Chop Move will almost certainly protect anyway. This is a narrow exception; unrelated Play Clues and Save Clues still have to be extinguished.

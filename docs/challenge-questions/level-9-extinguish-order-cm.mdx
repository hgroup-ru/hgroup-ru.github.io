<TabItem value="question">

It is the Early Game. Alice acts now, Bob acts next, and Cathy acts after Bob. Bob has two 1s with the same number clue. Cathy's red 5 is on chop, and saving it is the only remaining normal Save Clue. No other mandatory Early Game action remains.

Must Alice give the 5 Save before the team can make its first completely unknown discard?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Bob can protect Cathy's chop with an **Order Chop Move** before Cathy reaches her turn: by playing the newer of his two equally clued 1s, Bob skips over one 1 and tells the next player, Cathy, to Chop Move one card. Cathy's red 5 will therefore become Chop Moved before it is in danger of being discarded. Level 9 explicitly excludes cards that will almost certainly be protected by an upcoming Order Chop Move from the Early Game requirement to extinguish every clue.

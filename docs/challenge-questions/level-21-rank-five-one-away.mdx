<TabItem value="question">

It is Mid-Game. Red 3 is played. Cathy's hand from newest to oldest is blue 4, green 3, blue 4, blue 5, blue 1. Alice clues number 5 to Cathy, focusing the blue 5 exactly one-away-from-chop. Bob expected Alice to clue Cathy's 1. At Level 21, may Bob interpret the rank-5 clue as a Play Clue on the 5? What should he do?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

No. In the **Chop Move Ignition Interaction**, Play Clue interpretations for a rank-5 clue focused on a 5 exactly one-away-from-chop are turned off. Bob therefore treats the 5 as Chop Moved and reacts to the Chop Move Ignition by blind-playing Finesse Position; in the source position this is red 4. Cathy then recognizes the CMI and blind-plays her slot 5. The blind-play is about the card being Chop Moved, not about connecting directly to the 5.

<TabItem value="question">

It is not End-Game, and a 5 is exactly one-away from chop. Alice gives a **rank-5** clue. The 5 happens to be playable right now, and a 5 Stall is impossible. May Bob treat the clue as a direct Play Clue on the playable 5 and skip the Chop Move interpretation?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** At Level 21, rank-5 Play Clue interpretations on a 5 exactly one-away-from-chop are turned off outside End-Game. Bob must treat it as a 5's Chop Move interaction even if the physical 5 is already playable, and if Chop Moving that card is nonsensical without another reason, that may trigger Chop Move Ignition. This special interaction is turned off in End-Game.

</TabItem>

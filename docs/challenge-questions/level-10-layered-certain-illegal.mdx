<TabItem value="question">

Red and green are both stacked through 2. Alice has a red-clued card that she knows is `r3` or `r5`. Cathy has an untouched `r4`. Bob's first two cards are `g3` and `r3`.

Would a red Play Clue from Alice to Cathy's `r4` be valid?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

No. The clue would require Bob to play `g3` first and then `r3`, while Alice herself could still hold `r3`. That is a **Layered Certain Finesse**, and it is illegal. The Certain Finesse rule deliberately forbids putting an unrelated playable layer in front of a card that could be duplicated in the initiator's own hand.

</TabItem>

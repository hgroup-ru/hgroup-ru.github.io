<TabItem value="question">

Alice makes a Priority deviation that would appear to Finesse Cathy. Bob sees the actual next connecting card in Cathy's hand, but it is off her Finesse Position. The direct clue Bob must give to that connector would, in isolation, look like a Save Clue because of where it sits in Cathy's hand.

May Bob avoid the clue and let Cathy blind-play because the direct clue looks like a Save?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A visible connector in a non-Finesse Position turns off the Priority Finesse and commits the team to a **Load Clue**. It is still a Load Clue even if its shape would otherwise look like a Save Clue. Cathy rewinds her Priority-related notes and interprets the Load Clue as a normal Play Clue, not as permission to continue the false blind-play.

</TabItem>

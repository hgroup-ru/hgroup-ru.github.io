<TabItem value="question">

Alice chooses between two playable cards. One is fully known and modestly better according to ordinary Priority. The other is globally unknown. Playing the unknown card loses no clear Tempo, abandons no obvious connection, and is only slightly less attractive than the known play.

Should the team force a Trust Finesse interpretation merely because Alice chose the unknown card?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Playing an unknown card normally triggers nothing special. A Trust Finesse requires the choice to be **extremely suboptimal** without the hidden message. A small Priority preference is not enough to turn an ordinary unknown-card play into a forced Trust signal.

</TabItem>

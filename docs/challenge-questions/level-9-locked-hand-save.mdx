<TabItem value="question">

Bob has a Locked Hand. He has no higher-precedence clue available. Cathy's chop card is neither critical nor playable, but Bob can give it a color clue without making Cathy's entire hand Locked.

May Bob save that chop card as a Locked Hand Save?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Yes.** A Locked Hand Save may save **any** card on chop, not only a critical or playable card, provided no higher-precedence clue is available and the save itself does not Lock the recipient. If the clue would Lock Cathy, it would instead be interpreted as a normal Play Clue or Save Clue rather than as a Locked Hand Save.

</TabItem>

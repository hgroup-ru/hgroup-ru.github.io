<TabItem value="question">

Alice has red-2 Elimination Notes on slots 2 through 5. Bob gives number 3 touching slots 2, 3, and 4. The negative information removes the red-2 notes from those cards, leaving slot 5 as red 2. Does that newly determined chop position retroactively make slot 4 the focus of Bob's 3 clue?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Chop-focus is evaluated **before the clue is given**. At that moment the clue normally focuses the newest touched card, slot 2. The fact that the clue's negative information later singles out red 2 on slot 5 does not retroactively move the clue's focus to slot 4.

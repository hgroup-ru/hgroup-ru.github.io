<TabItem value="question">

Alice can plainly trigger an ordinary clue-based Double Ignition by re-cluing Cathy's globally known playable yellow 2 without touching a new card. Instead, she deliberately misplays her globally known-trash card. Bob, Cathy, and Donald each have a playable card in Finesse Position.

What extra information does the unnecessarily expensive choice communicate?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

It upgrades the message to a **Bomb Triple Ignition**. Spending a strike is worse than using the available clue-based Double Ignition, so choosing the bomb despite an obvious cheaper trigger is the extra signal. Bob, Cathy, and Donald should therefore each perform an Ignition rather than stopping after two blind-plays.

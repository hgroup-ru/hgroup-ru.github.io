<TabItem value="question">

At Level 14, Bob has two globally known-trash cards with equivalent clue information. Which one is he normally expected to discard, and what does skipping it mean?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob normally discards the **leftmost** known-trash card. If he instead discards the second-leftmost equivalent trash card, the skip is meaningful and causes a Trash Order Chop Move on the next player. Do not import the Level 16 UTD/UDD focus-first exception backward into this Level 14 default.

</TabItem>

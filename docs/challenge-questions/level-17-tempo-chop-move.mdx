<TabItem value="question">

Use the Duplicitous Tempo position shown below. Alice was **not** in a valid stalling situation, and the fill-in on Bob's green 2 gives only one unit of Tempo on a Promptable card, so that Tempo is not Valuable. After Bob discovers that the focused play was the duplicate red 2, does the clue also move Bob's chop?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Yes.** Duplicitous Tempo Clues use the **same Tempo Clue Chop Move conditions as ordinary Tempo Clues**. Here the clue cannot be explained as an allowed stall and its one unit of Tempo is not Valuable, so Bob also interprets it as a Tempo Clue Chop Move.

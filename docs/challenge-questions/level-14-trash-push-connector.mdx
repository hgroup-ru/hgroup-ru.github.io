<TabItem value="question">

Alice gives Donald a Trash Push by cluing the trash 1 on his chop. The pushed card on Donald's slot 3 is red 3, one-away-from-playable, and Bob has a known playable red 2. Can Bob defer the red 2 and allow Donald to reach his turn before the connection resolves?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A Trash Push on a one-away-from-playable card promises a connecting **Prompt or Finesse**, and that connection cannot be reverse: it must fully resolve **before the clued player gets a turn**. Bob should play red 2 before Donald is expected to demonstrate the pushed red 3.

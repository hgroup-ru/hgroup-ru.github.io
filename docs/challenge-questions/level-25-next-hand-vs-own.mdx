<TabItem value="question">

Alice has two fully-known playable cards. Playing blue 2 would immediately make Bob's globally-known blue 3 playable. Playing red 2 would instead make Alice's own globally-known red 3 playable. Neither card is a blind-play and no special exception applies.

Which card has Priority?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Blue 2.** A card that leads into a clued or known card in **someone else's hand** has higher Priority than a card that leads into the player's **own hand**. Alice should play blue 2 first; choosing red 2 would be a meaningful Priority deviation.

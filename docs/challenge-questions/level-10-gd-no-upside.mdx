<TabItem value="question">

All 1s are played. Alice has a clued red card that she has identified as `r2`. Bob's slot 1 is the other `r2`. There is no immediate danger elsewhere.

What is Alice's best move?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice should **play her `r2`**. A Gentleman's Discard is allowed here, but it only moves the same playable card to Bob and delays the stack with no compensating benefit. This is exactly the kind of unnecessary GD that loses Tempo.

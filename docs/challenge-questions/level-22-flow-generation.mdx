<TabItem value="question">

Alice had a known play at 0 clues but made an emergency discard instead, creating one clue. Bob has no known play or known safe discard. Cathy also has no safe action, and her yellow 5 on chop is critical because the other yellow 5 is already discarded.

What should Bob do with the generated clue?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob should **save Cathy's yellow 5**. In this branch Alice's discard is a **Generation Discard**, not a Scream aimed at Bob: Cathy has the important chop and no safe action, so Bob should use the newly generated clue for a Play Clue or Save Clue on that card rather than Chop Moving his own chop.

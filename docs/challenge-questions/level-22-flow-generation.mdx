<TabItem value="question">

Alice makes the emergency discard described by the Level 22 flowchart. Bob has no known play and no known safe discard. Cathy also has no known play or safe discard, but Cathy has a unique playable card, critical card, or unique 2 on chop. What does the flowchart call Alice's discard, and what should Bob do?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

This is a **Generation Discard**, not a Scream Discard Chop Move. Bob should use the generated clue to give a **Play Clue or Save Clue to Cathy's important chop card** rather than Chop Moving his own chop.

</TabItem>

<TabItem value="question">

Alice discards a playable 1 in a position where the action could look either like an ordinary Gentleman's Discard to another copy or like a Blaze Discard encoding a non-first Finesse Position. Which interpretation has precedence at Level 23?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Blaze Discard takes precedence.** Because 1s have three copies, a plain Gentleman's Discard can be ambiguous about a third copy on Finesse Position. The Level 23 convention resolves that ambiguity by preferring Blaze when the weird positional-transfer conditions fit.

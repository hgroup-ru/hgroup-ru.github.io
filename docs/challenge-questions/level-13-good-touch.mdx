<TabItem value="question">

Red 2 and blue 2 are played. Alice clues Cathy number 4, and Bob blind-plays blue 3. Donald already has a clued blue card that the team can see is blue 4, although Donald does not yet know its exact identity. Should Cathy conclude that her newly clued 4 is blue 4?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** If Cathy were also blue 4, Alice would have duplicated Donald's already-touched blue 4 and violated Good Touch Principle. That visible duplicate lets Cathy reject the superficially connecting blue-4 Finesse interpretation. The clue can therefore be a Good Touch Bluff on another legal one-away 4; in this position the intended identity is red 4.

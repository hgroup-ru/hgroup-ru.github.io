Red 2 and blue 2 are played. Alice clues Cathy number 4 on a physical red 4, and Bob blind-plays blue 3. Donald already has a touched blue card that is visibly blue 4 to the team. Should Cathy still conclude that she is blue 4?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** If Cathy were also blue 4, Alice would have duplicated Donald's already-touched blue 4 and violated Good Touch Principle. That visible duplicate rules out the superficially connecting blue-4 Finesse. Cathy can therefore interpret her card as the other legal one-away 4, red 4, and recognize the clue as a **Good Touch Bluff**.

<TabItem value="question">

Alice has exactly two cards left with Elimination Notes for a playable red 2. Her teammates were not busy and deliberately let the older noted card remain on chop through the previous turns without saving it.

What does Elimination Riding Deduction tell Alice?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

The red 2 **cannot be on the riding chop card**. If it were, an unbusy teammate would have protected it with a Play Clue or Save Clue. Alice can therefore jump ahead and blind-play the newer of the two noted cards as red 2. This deduction depends on both the Ride and the teammates not being busy.

</TabItem>

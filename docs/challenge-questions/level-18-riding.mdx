Alice has exactly two red-2 Elimination candidates left: slot 4 and chop slot 5. Her teammates were not busy and deliberately let slot 5 Ride for a full go-around. Which card can Alice play?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice can blind-play **slot 4** as an **Elimination Riding Deduction**. If red 2 were on chop, an unbusy teammate would have protected it with a Play Clue or Save Clue. Letting chop Ride eliminates slot 5, so the newer of the two candidates is the red 2.

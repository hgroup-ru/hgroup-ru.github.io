<TabItem value="question">

It is the first turn and all stacks are at 0. Alice gives Cathy a number 4 Play Clue to an untouched red 4. Bob sees no red cards in the other hands, so an ordinary Finesse would require Bob to blind-play red 1, red 2, and red 3. Cathy has a currently unplayable card on her Finesse Position, so a 4 Double Bluff cannot use that position.

Which position should Bob blind-play?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob should blind-play his **Fourth Finesse Position** as a **4 Charm**. A number clue avoids the Critical Color Bluff interpretation, the ordinary Finesse would require three blind-plays, and Cathy's unplayable Finesse Position rules out the normal 4 Double Bluff route. Those failures are exactly what make Bob fall through to the 4 Charm interpretation.

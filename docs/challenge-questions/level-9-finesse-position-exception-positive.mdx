It is the Early Game. The only remaining normal Play Clue would directly touch Cathy's playable red 1 on Finesse Position. Alice can instead 5 Stall Bob's off-chop green 5. If she does, Bob acts before Cathy and can clue Donald's red 2, genuinely Finessing Cathy to play red 1 before Cathy's normal turn.

May Alice use the 5 Stall under the Finesse Position Exception?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Yes.** This is the positive form of the Finesse Position Exception: only one Play Clue remains, it touches a card on Finesse Position, and that card can actually be gotten by another player before the recipient reaches their turn. Alice may 5 Stall instead of directly cluing the red 1, preserving the possibility for Bob to Finesse it.

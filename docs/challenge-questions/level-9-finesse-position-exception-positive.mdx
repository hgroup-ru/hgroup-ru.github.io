<TabItem value="question">

It is the Early Game and Alice starts her turn with 2 clues. The only remaining normal Play Clue would directly touch Cathy's playable red 1 on Finesse Position. Alice can instead 5 Stall Bob's off-chop green 5. Bob acts next, with 1 clue left, and can clue Donald's red 2 before Cathy acts. That clue can Finesse Cathy, who would then blind-play her red 1 on her own turn.

May Alice use the 5 Stall under the Finesse Position Exception?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Yes.** This is the positive form of the Finesse Position Exception: only one Play Clue remains, it touches a card on Finesse Position, and another player can actually Finesse that card before its owner acts. After Alice spends one of the 2 clues on the 5 Stall, Bob still has a clue available and acts before Cathy, so he can give the Finesse clue before Cathy's turn; Cathy then blind-plays the red 1 on her turn.

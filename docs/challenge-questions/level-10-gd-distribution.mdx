<TabItem value="question">

All 1s are played. Alice knows her red card is `r2`. Bob's slot 1 is the other `r2`, but Bob already has three known playable 2s in the same hand.

How should Alice move?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice should **play her own `r2`**. A GD is legal, but it would move yet another useful action into Bob's already-busy hand. That works against Team Distribution and adds delay without solving a problem.

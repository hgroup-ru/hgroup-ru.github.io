<TabItem value="question">

One red 2 is already discarded, and Cathy has the other red 2 on Finesse Position. Alice clues Bob's slot 1 with number 2; Bob can see that his touched 2 cannot be red 2, so he discards it as trash. Should Cathy now blind-play her red 2 as a Reverse Trash Finesse reaction?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Reverse Trash Finesses must contain a **potential Gentleman's Discard**. Here red 2 is critical, so the hypothetical case where Bob's touched card was red 2 could not be covered by discarding it. Therefore Cathy must not promote Bob's trash discard into a Reverse Trash Finesse.

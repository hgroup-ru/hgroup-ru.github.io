<TabItem value="question">

Earlier, Alice gave Bob a 2 Save touching three 2s; the chop 2 was the focus of that Save. Much later all 1s are played, so Bob knows all three 2s are playable but still does not know their colors. The saved chop 2 is not the leftmost 2.

Must Bob follow the normal same-rank left-to-right order?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Bob knows the original Save focus is the **more important** 2. That importance overrides the ordinary leftmost tie-breaker, so he plays the saved 2 first and then returns to the normal left-to-right order. Doing so must not be misread as a right-to-left Priority Finesse.

</TabItem>

<TabItem value="question">

A genuine No-Information re-clue causes Bob to blind-play red 1. If interpreting that blind-play as a Bluff would get only this single card and promise no red 2, may the team stop there and call it a one-card No-Information Bluff?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A No-Information clue **may** cause a Finesse that gets a single card, but a No-Information **Bluff can never get only one card**; it must get two. In the source line, that restriction forces the Finesse interpretation, and Bob can infer that he also has the connecting red 2 to blind-play on his next turn.

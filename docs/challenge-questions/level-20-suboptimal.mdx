<TabItem value="question">

Alice has a free choice between two ways to save a critical card. One clue is slightly less efficient on paper, but it avoids touching a possible duplicate already clued in Bob's hand. Should Bob automatically treat the chosen clue as a Suboptimal Finesse/Bluff because another clue scores a little better?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A Suboptimal move has to be **clearly, conspicuously worse**, not just technically inferior by a small metric. Good Touch concerns, useful negative information, or other context can justify the apparent loss. Only when the worse choice lacks a reasonable ordinary explanation should the team read the extra Play Clue/Finesse/Bluff message.

</TabItem>

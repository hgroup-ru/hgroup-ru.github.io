<TabItem value="question">

Red 1 is already played. Alice gives Cathy a number 4 Play Clue to a previously untouched red 4 that is not yet playable. Bob has no matching Prompt. From Bob's perspective, completing the ordinary Finesse would require him to blind-play red 2 and red 3 from his hand before Cathy's red 4 can play.

Should Bob skip those two blind-plays and instead treat the clue as a 4 Charm?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Two required blind-plays are still within the normal Finesse interpretation. The 4 Charm fallback becomes relevant when the apparent Finesse would require **three** blind-plays, after the earlier interpretations have been ruled out. Bob should follow the two-card Finesse rather than jump to his Fourth Finesse Position.

</TabItem>

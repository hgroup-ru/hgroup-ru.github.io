<TabItem value="question">

All 2s are played. Alice's red-clued card could initially be `r3`, `r4`, or `r5`. Alice gives Bob a red Play Clue on `r4`.

**After Alice's clue:**

<Start />

Bob discards. Cathy then blind-plays `r3` instead of Certain Discarding.

**After Cathy's play:**

<AfterThree />

Alice safely discards. Bob then plays his `r4` instead of Sarcastic Discarding it.

**When the turn returns to Alice:**

<Current />

What is Alice's correct move now?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice should **play her red-clued card as `r5`**.

- Cathy played `r3` instead of Certain Discarding it. Therefore Alice cannot hold `r3`; otherwise Cathy would have passed the duplicated `r3` back.
- Bob later played `r4` instead of Sarcastic Discarding it. Therefore Alice cannot hold `r4` either.
- The only remaining identity is `r5`, and red is now stacked through 4.

The original clue combined the Sarcastic Finesse and Certain Finesse mechanisms. That combination is a **Composition Finesse**.

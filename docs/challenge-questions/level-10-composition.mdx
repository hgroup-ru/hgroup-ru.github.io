<TabItem value="question">

All 2s are played. Alice's red-clued card could initially be `r3`, `r4`, or `r5`. Alice gives Bob a red Play Clue on `r4`.

**After Alice's clue:**

<Start />

Bob makes a safe unrelated discard. Cathy then blind-plays her Finesse Position, which turns out to be `r3`.

**After Cathy's play:**

<AfterThree />

Alice makes a safe discard. Bob then plays his clued `r4`.

**When the turn returns to Alice:**

<Current />

What is Alice's correct move now?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice should **play her red-clued card as `r5`**.

- Cathy's blind-play was `r3`. If Alice herself held `r3`, the Certain Finesse structure would allow Cathy to Certain Discard that duplicated `r3` instead of playing it. Cathy's play therefore rules out `r3` for Alice.
- Bob later played his clued `r4`. If Alice held `r4`, the Sarcastic Finesse structure would call for Bob to Sarcastic Discard that duplicate instead. Bob's play therefore rules out `r4` for Alice.
- The only remaining identity is `r5`, and red is now stacked through 4.

The original clue combined the Sarcastic Finesse and Certain Finesse mechanisms. That combination is a **Composition Finesse**.

</TabItem>

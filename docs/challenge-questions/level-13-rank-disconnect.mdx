<TabItem value="question">

On turn 1, Alice clues Cathy number 3 on one card. Bob blind-plays blue 1. What does Cathy learn beyond the positive rank clue?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

This is a **3 Bluff**. Rank 1 does not connect to rank 3, so Cathy does not read the blind-play as a Finesse. She knows the touched card is a 3, but gains no suit information from the Bluff.

<TabItem value="question">

On the first turn, Alice clued Cathy number 3, touching one card. Bob blind-played a blue 1. What does Cathy know about the touched card beyond the positive rank clue?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

This is a **3 Bluff**. A played 1 does not connect to a number 3 clue, so the clue is not a Finesse. Cathy knows the card is a 3 from the clue itself, but the Bluff gives her no suit information; she should keep it as any 3 rather than inventing a connecting suit.

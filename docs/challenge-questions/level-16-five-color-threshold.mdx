<TabItem value="question">

Alice gives a color Play Clue to a previously untouched 5. Bob sees that interpreting it as a Finesse would require him to blind-play **two** cards from his own hand before the 5 can play. Which interpretation takes precedence?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**5 Color Ejection.** If Bob would need two or more blind-plays from his own hand to fulfill the Finesse, the Finesse is considered unlikely and the clue instead ejects Bob's **Second Finesse Position**. Prompts do not count toward this threshold; only blind-plays do.

</TabItem>

<TabItem value="question">

The red stack is empty and Alice's hand is completely unclued. The team is not busy, yet Alice is allowed to discard her chop; it is revealed to be a red 2. Alice cannot see the other red 2 in Bob's or Cathy's hand. She draws a new card into slot 1.

What should Alice conclude, and which slots should she mark before continuing?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice should conclude that **the other red 2 was already in her hand** and write red-2 Elimination Notes on **slots 2, 3, 4, and 5**. Save Principle says that all 2s must be saved, so in an otherwise ordinary position the team would not knowingly let the only visible copy disappear. The newly drawn slot 1 cannot be the copy that explained the discard, so it is excluded from the notes.

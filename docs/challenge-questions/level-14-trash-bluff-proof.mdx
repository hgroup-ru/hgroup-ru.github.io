<TabItem value="question">

Alice is in Bluff Seat and wants to initiate a Trash Bluff. The proposed clue could still leave the recipient believing that the touched card is a normal future playable card even after the blind-play occurs.

Is that sufficient for a Trash Bluff?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Normal Bluffs take precedence over Trash Bluffs. A Trash Bluff is only available when the blind-play itself will **prove that the clued card is trash**. For example, with red played through 3, a red clue is not suitable because after a blind-play the touched card can still look like red 5; with red played through 4, the same kind of blind-play can prove that the touched red card is not the playable red 5. Trash Bluffs also require Bluff Seat.

</TabItem>

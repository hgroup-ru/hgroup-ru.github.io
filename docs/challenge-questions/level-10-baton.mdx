<TabItem value="question">

Red 1 is played and the team is out of clues. Alice has a clued red card that she has fully identified as `r3`. Bob's Finesse Position is the other `r3`; Alice has no other known safe discard.

How should Alice move?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice should **discard her known `r3` as a Baton Discard**. `r3` is not playable yet, so this is not a Gentleman's Discard. The matching card is exactly on Bob's Finesse Position, which makes the Baton transfer legal; it also lets Alice avoid risking an unknown chop while generating a clue token.

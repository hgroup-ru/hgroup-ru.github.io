<TabItem value="question">

Red 1 is played. Alice has a clued red card that she has fully identified as `r3`. Bob's first slot is the other `r3`.

How should Alice move?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice can **discard her known `r3` as a Baton Discard**. Because `r3` is not playable yet, this is a Baton Discard, not a Gentleman's Discard. The matching card is exactly on Bob's Finesse Position, so the transfer is valid.

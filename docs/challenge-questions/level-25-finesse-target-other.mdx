<TabItem value="question">

Alice deliberately plays a fully-known blue 2 instead of a higher-Priority fully-known card. Bob is next. Bob has no clued blue 3, but he can see a playable blue 3 on Cathy's Finesse Position.

Should Bob blind-play his own Finesse Position because he is the next player?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Bob can see the intended connector on **Cathy's** Finesse Position, so this is a Priority Finesse on Cathy. Bob does something unrelated; Cathy blind-plays her Finesse Position on her turn. Priority Finesse is not automatically assigned to the next player when the matching visible connector identifies someone else.

</TabItem>

<TabItem value="question">

Nothing was played. Alice gave Donald a number 4 Play Clue. Bob then blind-played the red 1. Cathy discarded. From Donald's perspective, 1 does not connect directly to 4.

Should Donald interpret the clue as a Bluff because of the rank disconnect?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** With empty stacks there is no one-away-from-playable 4, so a 4 cannot be a legal Bluff target here. The Bluff interpretation is impossible despite the disconnect. Donald must therefore keep the truthful Finesse interpretation: the red 1 begins a multi-card connection toward a red 4, requiring red 2 and red 3 before the clued card.

Donald can record that explicitly: red 2 and red 3 are the two Finesse cards leading into his red 4.

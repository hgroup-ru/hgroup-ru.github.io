<TabItem value="question">

Red is on 1 and green is on 2. Bob's chop already has red information. Alice now gives Bob a number 3 Play Clue touching a newer card and chop. Bob therefore knows that chop is red 3 and is not directly playable. He sees no red 2 in Alice's or Cathy's hand, and his own Finesse Position is completely unclued. The newer touched card could instead be the directly playable green 3, so Focus Inversion also looks possible.

What should Bob do first?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob should **blind-play his Finesse Position first**. The number 3 clue can be a Self-Finesse through Bob's own red 2 to the red 3 on chop. Level 12 explicitly gives rank Self-Finesses precedence over Focus Inversion, so Bob does not take the apparently simpler direct green-3 focus. If his Finesse Position were known not to be a 2, that Self-Finesse line would disappear and the Focus Inversion reading could take over.

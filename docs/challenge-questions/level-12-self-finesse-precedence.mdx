<TabItem value="question">

Red is on 1 and green is on 2. Bob's chop already has negative green information. Alice gives Bob a number 3 Play Clue touching a newer card and chop.

Should Bob read this as Focus Inversion and take the newer touched 3 as the focus, or blind-play his Finesse Position first?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob should **blind-play his Finesse Position first**. Focus Inversion would move the focus away from chop because chop is known not green, but Level 12 has a specific exception: a rank Self-Finesse takes precedence over Focus Inversion. Bob therefore tries the Finesse first.

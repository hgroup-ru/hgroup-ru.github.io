<TabItem value="question">

A rank clue can be read in two ways that both fit the visible state: one interpretation is a rank Self-Finesse, while the other would use Focus Inversion to move focus away from chop. Neither interpretation has been ruled out by card knowledge.

Which interpretation has precedence at level 12?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

The player should assume the **rank Self-Finesse**. Level 12 explicitly gives rank Self-Finesses precedence over Focus Inversion. This is a named exception to the usual Occam's Razor ordering, so the apparently simpler inverted-focus reading does not win merely by requiring less machinery.

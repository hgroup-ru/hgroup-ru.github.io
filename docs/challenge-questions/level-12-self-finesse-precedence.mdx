<TabItem value="question">

Bob receives a rank Play Clue that touches his chop and a newer card. From Bob's existing notes, the chop cannot be the straightforward playable focus, so Focus Inversion would normally move focus to the newer card. However, the same rank clue also forms a legal Self-Finesse on Bob, and nothing in his card knowledge rules that line out.

Which interpretation should Bob follow?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob should follow the **rank Self-Finesse**. Level 12 makes this a specific exception to the usual Occam's Razor ordering: when a rank Self-Finesse and Focus Inversion are both live, the Self-Finesse has precedence. Bob should not choose the apparently simpler inverted-focus reading merely because it requires less machinery.

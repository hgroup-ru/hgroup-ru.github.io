<TabItem value="question">

All 1s are played. Cathy already knows that her red-clued card is the playable red 2. Alice has a known safe discard, but instead of discarding she gives Cathy the same red clue again, touching no new cards.

How should the team interpret Alice's clue?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

It is a **Replay Double Ignition**. Re-cluing a globally known playable card is normally useful only as a Stall; here Alice can safely discard, so that explanation is absent. Because the clue also introduces no new cards, the useless re-given Play Clue carries the deeper Double Ignition message. Replay Double Ignition is not restricted to End-Game.

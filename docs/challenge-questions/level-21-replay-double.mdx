<TabItem value="question">

Alice re-clues a globally known playable card. The clue touches no new cards. In case A, Alice is in a valid stalling situation and cannot discard. In case B, she is not in a stalling situation. How should the team distinguish the two cases? Would the interpretation change merely because this happens early rather than late in the game?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

In case A, the ordinary explanation is a **Stall**. In case B, the otherwise useless re-given Play Clue communicates a **Replay Double Ignition**. RDI must not introduce new cards, but it can be performed throughout the game; unlike Trash Double Ignition, it is not restricted to near the end.

</TabItem>

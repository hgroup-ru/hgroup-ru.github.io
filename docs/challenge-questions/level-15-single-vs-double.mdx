Red 1 is played. Alice clues red to Donald's red 2. Bob blind-plays an unrelated blue 1 from Finesse Position. Cathy has a playable yellow 1 on her own Finesse Position. Should Cathy also blind-play it as a Double Bluff continuation?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Red 2 is a legal ordinary Bluff target here. Bob's single blind-play can therefore resolve the clue as a normal Bluff, so Cathy has no reason to add a second blind-play. Double Bluffs are normally initiated with a non-trash card that is **not** a legal Bluff target.

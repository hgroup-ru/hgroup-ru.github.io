<TabItem value="question">

Red is stacked through 2. Cathy has a red-clued card that she knows is `r3` or `r5`. Cathy gives Bob a red Play Clue on `r4`, and Bob then makes a safe unrelated discard. Alice now reacts and can see that Cathy's ambiguous red card is actually `r5`.

What should Alice do?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice should **blind-play her Finesse Position as `r3`**. The clue is a **Certain Finesse** in structure, but Cathy does not actually hold the duplicated `r3`, so there is nothing to pass back with a Certain Discard. The promised blind card is the connecting `r3` and should play.

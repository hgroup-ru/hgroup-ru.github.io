<TabItem value="question">

A Double Ignition is triggered and three teammates visibly have playable cards on Finesse Position. Bob does not already have a known-playable card. Which two players are designated to blind-play?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

The **first Ignition is Bob**, and the **second is the last player with a playable Finesse Position**. If Bob already had a known-playable card, the first Ignition would move to the first player who did not already have a known play. This fixed targeting prevents an Ambiguous Double Ignition from producing competing blind-plays.

</TabItem>

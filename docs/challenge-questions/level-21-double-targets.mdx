<TabItem value="question">

A Double Ignition is triggered. Bob, Cathy, and Donald can each see that they have a playable card in Finesse Position. Bob already has another known-playable card in hand; Cathy does not; Donald is the last player among the candidates with a playable Finesse Position card. Who performs the two Ignitions?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

The first Ignition goes to **Cathy**, the first player after Bob who does not already have a known-playable card. Normally the first Ignition must be on Bob, but Bob's known-playable card activates the stated exception. The second Ignition goes to **Donald**, because it must be on the last player with a playable card.

The previous clue has already been identified as a Double Ignition. Bob, Cathy, and Donald each have a playable card in Finesse Position. Bob also has a different card that is globally known playable.

Which two players are supposed to blind-play?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Cathy and Donald.** The first Ignition normally belongs to Bob, but Bob already has another known-playable card, so the first Ignition moves to the first later player without one: Cathy. The second Ignition belongs to the **last** player with a playable Finesse Position card, which is Donald.

<TabItem value="question">

Red 2 and blue 2 are played. Alice gave Cathy a number 4 Play Clue. Bob immediately blind-played the blue 3. Cathy's clued 4 already had negative blue information.

Does the apparent 3→4 connection make this a Finesse?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A rank connection would normally point to the blue 4, but Cathy's negative blue information makes that truthful Finesse interpretation impossible. The clue must instead be a **Bluff**. Among Cathy's remaining possibilities, the red 4 is the one-away-from-playable 4 that can legally be the Bluff target.

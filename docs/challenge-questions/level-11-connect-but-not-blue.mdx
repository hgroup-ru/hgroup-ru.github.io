**No.** A rank connection would normally point to the blue 4, but Cathy's negative blue information makes that truthful Finesse interpretation impossible. The clue must instead be a **Bluff**. Among Cathy's remaining possibilities, the red 4 is the one-away-from-playable 4 that can legally be the Bluff target. Cathy therefore records **r4** on the clued card.

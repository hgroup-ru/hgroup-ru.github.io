Red 1 and blue 2 are played. Cathy already has an unknown-color clued 3. Alice gives Cathy a Play Clue to a new 4, and Bob blind-plays red 2. Must Cathy immediately play her clued 3 as red 3?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A simple Bluff is still possible because the new 4 could be blue 4, which is one-away-from-playable. A red Finesse + Prompt line is also possible if the cards are red 3 and red 4. Under Occam's Razor, Cathy does not promote the more complicated line to certainty. This is the Hard Bluff ambiguity: she keeps both possibilities and does not immediately play the 3.

<TabItem value="question">

Cathy is still waiting to play a 5-Pulled card. On her turn she gives a Finesse to Donald instead of resolving her own pulled card. How should the table classify Cathy's Finesse, and where would a Certain Discard made in response apply?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Cathy's move is a **Certain Finesse**. Any Finesse performed while the player is still 5 Pulled is treated as certain. Consequently, a Certain Discard performed in response refers to **Cathy's 5-Pulled card**, not to some unrelated card in her hand.

<TabItem value="question">

All 1s are played. Cathy's hand from newest to oldest is blue 4, blue 1, red 2, blue 4. Alice clues number 1 to Cathy's slot 2, which would normally Trash Chop Move slots 3 and 4. Bob can see that the left Chop Moved card is the playable red 2, while the right one is a blue 4 matching Cathy's other untouched blue 4.

Should Bob treat this strange two-card Chop Move as an Ignition, and what should happen next?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Yes.** A multi-card Chop Move Ignition requires the **leftmost Chop Moved card to be playable** and the **rightmost Chop Moved card to be trash or a copy of an unclued card in the same hand**. This position satisfies both conditions. Bob therefore blind-plays blue 2 for the Ignition; Cathy then blind-plays red 2 and treats the rightmost card as known trash. When that rightmost card is later discarded as blue 4, the false-trash result creates Elimination Notes for the other copy.

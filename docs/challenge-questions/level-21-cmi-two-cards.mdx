<TabItem value="question">

All 1s are played. Cathy's hand from newest to oldest is blue 4, blue 1, red 2, blue 4. Alice clues number 1 to Cathy's slot 2, which would normally Trash Chop Move slots 3 and 4. Under what two conditions is a Chop Move Ignition with two or more Chop Moved cards valid, and how does this source position satisfy them?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Both conditions are required: **the leftmost Chop Moved card must be playable**, and **the rightmost Chop Moved card must be trash or a copy of an unclued card in the same hand**. Here slot 3 is the playable red 2, while slot 4 is blue 4, a copy of Cathy's other unclued blue 4. Bob therefore blind-plays blue 2 for the Ignition; Cathy then blind-plays red 2 and treats the rightmost card as known trash. When that rightmost card is later discarded as blue 4, the false-trash result creates Elimination Notes for the other copy.

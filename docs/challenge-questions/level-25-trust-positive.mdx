<TabItem value="question">

All 1s are played. Alice has a globally-known playable red 2 that leads directly into Bob's globally-known red 3. She also has a playable rank-2 card whose color is globally unknown, although Alice herself has enough private context to know that it is blue 2. Bob has blue 3 on his Finesse Position. Alice plays the globally-unknown 2 instead of the red 2.

Can this unknown-card play send a special signal even though unknown cards normally do not trigger Priority?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Yes.** Giving up the obvious red 2 → Bob's red 3 teamwork is extremely suboptimal unless Alice is communicating something. Her globally-unknown blue 2 can therefore initiate a **Trust Finesse** on Bob's blue 3. Trust moves are the narrow exception to the usual rule that playing an unknown card sends no Priority signal.

</TabItem>

<TabItem value="question">

Alice could directly Play Clue the card she wants, but instead considers a Trash Chop Move whose normal effect would get that same card. There is no side benefit, so the move would otherwise qualify as Unnecessary.

May Alice deliberately use this as an Unnecessary Trash Chop Move and expect the team to add the Level 24 extra meaning?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** An **Unnecessary Trash Chop Move is illegal**. It would be indistinguishable from a Chop Move Ignition, so the convention explicitly forbids this construction instead of asking the team to guess between the two meanings.

</TabItem>

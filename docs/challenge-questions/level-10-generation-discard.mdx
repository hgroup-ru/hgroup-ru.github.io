<TabItem value="question">

All 1s are played and there are 0 clues. Alice has a known playable `r2`. Bob has no clued cards. Cathy's `y5` is untouched on chop.

How should Alice move?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice should **discard her unknown chop card as a Generation Discard** instead of playing `r2`. That immediately creates a clue for Bob, who can 5 Save Cathy's `y5` before Cathy acts. If Alice simply plays, Bob has no clue to spend; relying on Cathy to choose a clue instead of discarding is exactly the kind of hidden-hand assumption the line should avoid.

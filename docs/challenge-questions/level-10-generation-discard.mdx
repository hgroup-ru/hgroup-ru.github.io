<TabItem value="question">

All 1s are played and there are 0 clues. Alice has a known playable `r2`. Bob's Finesse Position is the other `r2`, and Bob has no clued cards. Cathy's `y5` is untouched on chop.

How should Alice move?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice should **discard her unknown chop card as a Generation Discard** rather than play or Gentleman's Discard the `r2`. The discard immediately creates a clue, so Bob can 5 Save Cathy's `y5` before Cathy acts. If Alice plays `r2`, Bob has no clue to spend. If Alice Gentleman's Discards `r2`, Bob must use his turn to play the transferred `r2`, so Cathy's `y5` still reaches her turn unclued. Generation Discard is the line that creates the clue without consuming Bob's rescue turn.

</TabItem>

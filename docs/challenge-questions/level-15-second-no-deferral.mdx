<TabItem value="question">

Alice clued Donald's red 4, and Bob has just blind-played blue 1. Cathy cannot rule out that the clue is a Double Bluff, but she sees a strong clue she would like to give now. May she postpone her own blind-play until her next turn?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** If Cathy cannot rule out a Double Bluff after Bob's blind-play, she should **never defer**. A later blind-play would no longer be interpreted as belonging to the original Double Bluff and would create unrecoverable information desynchronization.

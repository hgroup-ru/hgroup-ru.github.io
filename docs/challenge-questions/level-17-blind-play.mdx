<TabItem value="question">

Red 2 and blue 2 are played. Alice has a globally known red 3. Alice clues blue to Cathy, touching a valuable blue 4 as a Play Clue. Bob blind-plays his Finesse Position expecting blue 3, but it is red 3 and successfully plays. The blue 4 supplies enough clue value. Is blue 4 now promised to be playable through another hidden connection?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** This is a **Duplicitous Blind-Play**. As long as the clue already meets Minimum Clue Value Principle from the valuable card it secures, the surprising duplicate blind-play is the mechanism used to obtain that value. It does not call for another Prompt or blind-play, and Cathy's blue 4 is not thereby promised playable.

<TabItem value="question">

It is the Early Game. Bob has two completely untouched red 1s. The only apparent Play Clue left is a red clue to Bob, which would touch both copies at once. No other mandatory Early Game action remains.

Must Alice give the red clue before the team makes its first completely unknown discard?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Alice is expected to notice that the red clue would duplicate the same identity and violate Good Touch Principle. The requirement to extinguish every Early Game clue does not include Play Clues that violate Good Touch. The team can wait for one copy to leave Bob's hand instead of spending a clue to touch both red 1s.

<TabItem value="question">

Red 2 and yellow 1 are played. Alice has a globally known playable red 3, while Bob has no useful action. Cathy's yellow 2 is on chop. Both Alice and Bob could give Cathy the same number-2 Save Clue, but Alice gives it and Bob then discards. How should Cathy interpret the clued yellow 2?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Cathy should treat it as an **Occupied Play Clue** and play the yellow 2. Alice had a playable card, Bob could have given the exact same clue, and Alice's choice to "steal" that clue left Bob with nothing useful to do. Those are the conditions that make Alice **Occupied**, so the ordinary 2 Save gains one extra meaning and becomes a Play Clue.

If Alice had been the only player who could reasonably give the clue, delaying her own play would be ordinary teamwork and would **not** create an Occupied meaning.

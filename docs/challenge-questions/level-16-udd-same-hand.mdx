<TabItem value="question">

Alice gives Cathy a red clue that appears to duplicate a red 2 already visible in Donald's hand. None of the normal duplication exceptions applies. Can Bob treat this as an Unknown Dupe Discharge merely because a duplicate exists somewhere at the table?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Unknown Dupe Discharge applies only when the two duplicated cards are in the **same person's hand**. Duplicating a card in somebody else's hand cannot be used to initiate UDD; the clue must have some other interpretation.

</TabItem>

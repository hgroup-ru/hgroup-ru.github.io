Should Bob blind-play it as a possible Bluff?

<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Bob can see a truthful Finesse interpretation: Cathy can blind-play the blue 2 and connect to Donald's blue 3. Under Bob's Truth Principle, before any blind-play Bob assumes a real Finesse over a Bluff whenever the truthful interpretation is possible. He therefore does not blind-play his own Finesse Position, even if that card would happen to play successfully.

<TabItem value="question">

Alice just discarded a red 4. Bob appears to be in a Double Discard situation for red 4 and has an obvious legal 5 Stall available, yet Bob discards anyway. Alice cannot see another red 4 outside her own hand. What should Alice conclude?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob's discard says that he **sees the other red 4**, so he knew he was not truly Double Discarding. Since Alice cannot see that copy elsewhere, she should place red-4 Elimination Notes across her hand. This is Double Discard Elimination: the trigger is another player's otherwise impossible discard.

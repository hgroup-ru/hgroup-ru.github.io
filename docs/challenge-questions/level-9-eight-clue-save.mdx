<TabItem value="question">

The team climbed back to 8 clues in the Mid-Game. Alice has no known playable card and no higher-precedence clue. Cathy has an ordinary non-critical card in slot 3 that Alice would like to preserve. May Alice save it with an 8 Clue Save?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Yes.** In this Mid-Game position, an 8 Clue Save can save a card on or off chop when no higher-precedence clue is available. It can use either color or number.

Two boundaries matter outside this position: the convention does **not** apply on the first turn of the game, and a clue to slot 1 is interpreted as a normal Play Clue or Save Clue instead.

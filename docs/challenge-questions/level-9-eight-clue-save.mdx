<TabItem value="question">

The team climbed back to 8 clues in the Mid-Game. Alice has no higher-precedence clue. Cathy has an ordinary non-critical card in slot 3 that Alice would like to preserve. May Alice save it with an 8 Clue Save? Would the same convention apply on the first turn of the game or to a card in slot 1?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Yes in the stated Mid-Game position.** An 8 Clue Save can save any card on or off chop as long as it is not in slot 1 and no higher-precedence clue is available. It can use either color or number. The convention does **not** apply on the first turn of the game, and a clue to slot 1 is interpreted as a normal Play Clue or Save Clue instead.

</TabItem>

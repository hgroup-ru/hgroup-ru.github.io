<TabItem value="question">

Red is stacked through 2. Cathy has a red-clued card that, from her information, is `r3` or `r5`. Cathy gave Bob a red Play Clue on `r4`; Bob then took an unrelated safe discard. Alice is now the reacting player. Alice can see that Cathy's ambiguous red card is actually `r3`.

What should Alice do?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice should **Certain Discard her Finesse Position**. This is a **Certain Finesse**: because Cathy really holds the potentially duplicated `r3`, a legal clue cannot require Alice to play through an unrelated layer first. Alice can therefore be certain that her current Finesse Position is the duplicated `r3` that should be passed back to Cathy.

<TabItem value="question">

Bob receives a Play Clue and then hesitates by discarding a safe chop. Cathy has one already-clued playable card in her hand that could be the connector Bob is waiting for. She also has an unclued playable card on her Finesse Position that could be a different valid connector to the same clued card.

Can Cathy safely blind-play her Finesse Position just because Bob hesitated?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A Hesitation Blind-Play is unsafe when the hesitation could be for **either a clued card in your hand or your Finesse Position card**. Bob's pause does not tell Cathy which of the two connections he is allowing. Guessing the Finesse Position could misplay a critical card, so Cathy must not act on the hesitation as if it uniquely selected that slot.

</TabItem>

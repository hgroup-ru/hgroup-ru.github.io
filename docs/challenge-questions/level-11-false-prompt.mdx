<TabItem value="question">

Red 1 and blue 1 are played. Bob has a card clued only as a 2. Alice gives Donald a red Play Clue on his red 3. Bob sees no red 2 elsewhere, so the clue makes his unknown 2 look Prompted as the red 2. In reality Bob's card is the blue 2.

May Alice intentionally use this fake Prompt as a Bluff?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No. Bluff Prompts / Prompt Bluffs are illegal.** A Prompt must be truthful. If Bob plays the blue 2, the successful play does not immediately tell the rest of the team that Alice's red connection was false; another player can still infer a missing red 2 and misplay. The misinformation therefore fails the Lie Principle as well as the rule that Prompts must be the truth.

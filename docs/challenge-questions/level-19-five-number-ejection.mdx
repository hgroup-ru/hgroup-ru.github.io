It is the first turn. Alice clues Cathy's slot-1 5 while a normal Play Clue is available, so this is not a Stall. Cathy's slot 2 is red 3: not playable and two-away-from-playable. What must Bob do, and what information is recorded about Cathy's red 3?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob plays his **Second Finesse Position** as a **5 Number Ejection**. The adjacent red 3 is too far away for a Pull Finesse but is not trash. After the Ejection, Cathy's slot 2 is marked **Chop Moved** and **two-or-more-away-from-playable** for the whole team.

<TabItem value="question">

All 2s were played. Alice gave Cathy a red Play Clue touching one card. Bob immediately blind-played his Finesse Position, expecting the connecting red 3; it was the blue 3 and played successfully.

How should the team interpret Alice's clue now?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

This is a **Bluff**. Bob's blue 3 does not connect to the red clue, so Cathy's Connecting Principle rules out the ordinary Finesse interpretation. The clue target must instead be a red card that was one-away-from-playable when the clue was given: the red 4.

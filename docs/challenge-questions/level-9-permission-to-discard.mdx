<TabItem value="question">

It is still the Early Game. Alice acted directly before Bob. On Bob's turn, every normal Play Clue and Save Clue has been extinguished except for the yellow 1 that Bob could clue in Alice's hand. There are no other mandatory Early Game actions remaining.

Must Bob clue Alice before he may discard?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Permission to Discard is the important exception for the previous player's hand. Bob is not required to clue Alice, because Alice acted directly before him. He may clue her if he wants, or he may discard. This exception does not make arbitrary other visible Play Clues or Save Clues optional.

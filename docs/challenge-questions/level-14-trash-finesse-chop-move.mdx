<TabItem value="question">

Alice's number-1 clue to Donald's slot 2 looks like a Play Clue on red 1 or yellow 1, but Cathy blind-plays red 1 from Finesse Position. Donald now knows that his touched 1 is trash. Which of Donald's other cards are Chop Moved by the Trash Finesse?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Slots 3 and 4.** A Trash Finesse is simultaneously a Finesse and a Trash Chop Move: the recipient Chop Moves **all cards to the right of the trash card**. The touched trash on slot 2 itself is not the moved region.

<TabItem value="question">

A Trash Finesse has just been demonstrated: another player blind-played the card that the recipient originally expected their newly touched card to be. The trash card itself is in slot 2, with three cards to its right.

What should the recipient conclude about the touched card and those cards to its right?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

The touched card is **trash**, and every card to its right is **Chop Moved**. At Level 14 a Trash Finesse combines the information of a Finesse with the movement of a Trash Chop Move. Later Level 24 rules add a special refinement when the trash card was already on chop, but that refinement is not imported backward here.

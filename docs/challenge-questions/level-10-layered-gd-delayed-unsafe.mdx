<TabItem value="question">

Red is stacked through 2 and green is empty. Alice knows her red card is `r3`. Bob, who acts immediately after Alice, has `g2` in slot 1 and the other `r3` in slot 2. Cathy has a known `g1`, but she acts after Bob.

Can Alice discard `r3` as a Layered Gentleman's Discard here?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

No. Bob acts before Cathy can play `g1`. If Alice creates the layered promise, Bob will treat his first card as the playable layer and blind-play `g2` immediately, while green is still on 0. The `g2` misplays. The same cards are safe in the opposite player order, but unsafe here.

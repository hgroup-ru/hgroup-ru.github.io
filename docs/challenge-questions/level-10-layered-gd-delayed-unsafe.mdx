<TabItem value="question">

Red is stacked through 2 and green is empty. Alice knows her red card is `r3`. Bob, who acts immediately after Alice, has `g2` in slot 1 and the other `r3` in slot 2. Cathy has a known `g1`, but she acts after Bob.

Alice considers discarding `r3` as a Layered Gentleman's Discard. Is that move valid?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

No. As in the previous question, `g2` is not a legal playable layer when the discard is made, so the line would rely on a forbidden Patch Gentleman's Discard. Here the failure is immediate as well: Bob acts before Cathy can play `g1`, interprets slot 1 as the layer, and blind-plays `g2` while green is still on 0. It misplays.

<TabItem value="question">

No completely unknown card has been discarded yet. Bob discarded a card that he knew was trash, and later Cathy misplayed a card. Is the Early Game over?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** At level 9, the Early Game ends only when the first **completely unknown** card is discarded. A known-trash discard does not end it, and a misplay does not end it either. Special transfer discards likewise do not end the Early Game.

</TabItem>

<TabItem value="question">

No completely unknown card has been discarded yet. Earlier, Bob discarded a card he knew was trash, and Cathy later misplayed a card. Donald now has no normal Play Clue, Save Clue, or 5 Stall, but he could give a Tempo Clue that he would be happy to use as a Mid-Game stall.

Can Donald treat the game as Mid-Game and give the Tempo Clue merely to avoid discarding?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Neither event ended the Early Game. It ends only when the first **completely unknown** card is discarded; a known-trash discard and a misplay do not count. Donald therefore cannot use the Mid-Game Tempo Clue Stall interpretation here. If he gives the Tempo Clue, it keeps its ordinary interpretation; if he does not want that interpretation and no required Early Game clue remains, he must discard.

</TabItem>

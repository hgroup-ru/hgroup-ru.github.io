<TabItem value="question">

Red is stacked through 2. Alice knows her red card is `r3`. Cathy has `g2` in slot 1 and the other `r3` in slot 2, but green has not started yet. Bob, who acts before Cathy, has a known `g1`.

Alice considers discarding `r3` as a Layered Gentleman's Discard. Is that move valid?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

No. The card in front of the transferred `r3` is `g2`, which is not a playable layer when the discard is made. Alice cannot rely on Bob's later `g1` play to patch that layer. Advanced rules call this a Patch Gentleman's Discard and explicitly forbid it. The fact that this particular turn order would make `g2` playable before Cathy acts does not make the original transfer legal.

<TabItem value="question">

Red is stacked through 2. Alice knows her red card is `r3`. Cathy has `g2` in slot 1 and the other `r3` in slot 2, but green has not started yet. Bob, who acts before Cathy, has a known `g1`.

How should Alice move?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice can discard `r3` as a Layered Gentleman's Discard. Bob acts before Cathy and plays his known `g1`, so by the time Cathy acts her `g2` has become playable. Cathy can then follow the ordinary layered interpretation—play `g2` first, and keep the transferred `r3` behind it. The turn order makes the delayed layer safe.

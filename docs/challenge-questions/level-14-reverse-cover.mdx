<TabItem value="question">

Alice clues number 2 to Bob's slot 1. From Bob's perspective it looks like the final red 2, but Bob sees another red 2 on Cathy's Finesse Position, so Reverse Trash Finesse is possible. What single action covers both the normal-Play-Clue case and the Reverse Trash Finesse case?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob should **immediately discard the touched 2**. If it is trash, the discard demonstrates the Reverse Trash Finesse and Cathy can react. If it is actually red 2, the discard becomes a **Gentleman's Discard** on Cathy. Playing cannot safely cover both possibilities.

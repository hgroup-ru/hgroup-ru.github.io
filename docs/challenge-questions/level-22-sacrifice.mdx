<TabItem value="question">

Bob's hand is Locked. One clued card will be useful later but is non-critical because another copy exists. The game is in a dire position, so Bob discards that clued card. Must the team interpret the discard as Sarcastic Discard or Gentleman's Discard? How does it distinguish this move from a Generation Discard?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

No. A Locked player may intentionally discard a non-critical clued card as a **Sacrifice Discard**, and in that emergency it does **not** imply Sarcastic Discard or Gentleman's Discard. A non-critical discard by a Locked player can also be a Generation Discard; teammates distinguish them from the state of the game. Sacrifice Discards are very rare and normally belong only to very dire situations.

</TabItem>

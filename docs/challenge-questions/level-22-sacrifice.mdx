<TabItem value="question">

Bob's hand is Locked. One clued card is useful later but non-critical because another copy exists. In a dire position Bob discards that clued card to free his hand. Must teammates interpret the discard as Sarcastic Discard or Gentleman's Discard?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A Locked player may deliberately discard a non-critical clued card as a **Sacrifice Discard**, and in that context it does not imply Sarcastic Discard or Gentleman's Discard. Depending on the game, a locked-player discard could instead be Generation Discard; teammates distinguish those by the surrounding emergency and clue-generation needs.

</TabItem>

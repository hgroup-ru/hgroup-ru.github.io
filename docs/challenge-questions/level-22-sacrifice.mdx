<TabItem value="question">

Bob's hand is Locked in a very bad position. His clued red 3 will be useful later, but it is non-critical because Cathy visibly holds the other red 3. Bob deliberately discards his clued red 3 to unlock his hand.

Must the team interpret that discard as Sarcastic Discard or Gentleman's Discard?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A Locked player may discard a non-critical clued card as a **Sacrifice Discard** in a dire situation. That emergency discard does not imply Sarcastic Discard or Gentleman's Discard. The key facts are that Bob is Locked, the discarded card is non-critical, and the position is bad enough to justify the rare sacrifice.

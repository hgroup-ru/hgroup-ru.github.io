<TabItem value="question">

A genuine No-Information re-clue first causes Bob to blind-play an unrelated green 1. Cathy then sees Bob lacks the expected green 2, so she blind-plays her own Finesse Position expecting that connection, but her card is unrelated yellow 1. What does the table learn from these two non-connecting blind-plays?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

The clue is revealed as a **No-Information Double Bluff (NIDB)** rather than a Finesse. The two blind-plays satisfy the rule that a No-Information Bluff must get two cards. Because neither card actually connects to the touched 3, Cathy's 3 remains in the same superposition as before.

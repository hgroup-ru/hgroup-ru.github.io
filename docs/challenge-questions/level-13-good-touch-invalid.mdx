Red 2 and blue 2 are played. Alice gives Cathy a number 4 clue on a physical red 4. Bob blind-plays blue 3. No already-touched blue 4 is visible elsewhere. Is this a safe Bluff?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Rank 3 connects to rank 4, so Cathy reads the line as a Finesse and concludes that her card is blue 4. She would misplay the physical red 4 as blue 4. Without information that rules out blue 4, Alice must not give this Bluff.

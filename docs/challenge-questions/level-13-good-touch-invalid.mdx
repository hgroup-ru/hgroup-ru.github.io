<TabItem value="question">

Red 2 and blue 2 are played. Alice gives Cathy a number 4 clue touching a red 4. Bob blind-plays blue 3. No already-clued blue 4 is visible elsewhere.

Is this a safe Bluff on Cathy's red 4?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Number 3 connects to number 4, so Cathy reads Bob's blue 3 as a Finesse and concludes that her touched 4 is blue 4. She would then misplay the physical red 4 as blue 4. Without the Good Touch elimination supplied by an already-touched blue 4 elsewhere, Alice must not give this Bluff.

</TabItem>

<TabItem value="question">

Alice makes a genuine last-resort Scream Discard. Bob and Cathy, the next two players in order, each already have a known playable card. Donald is the first player after them who does not have a safe action.

How many Chop Moves does the Scream create?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

It creates **three Chop Moves**: Bob, Cathy, and Donald all Chop Move. The Echo Scream bounces off each consecutive player who already has a known play or known safe discard, then reaches the next player. The same bounce rule also works when the underlying Scream Discard is performed with known trash.

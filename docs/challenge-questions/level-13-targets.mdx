<TabItem value="question">

Green 1 is played, but green 2 is not. Alice is in Bluff Seat for Bob. Cathy has a completely untouched green 3. Alice gives Cathy a number 3 Play Clue, and Bob immediately blind-plays an unrelated blue 1.

Can Cathy's green 3 be the legal target of this Bluff even though it was two-away-from-playable when Alice gave the clue?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Yes.** Level 13 adds the special **3 Bluff**: an untouched 3 may be a Bluff target while it is still two-away-from-playable. The exception works with either a color clue or a number clue. Bob's unrelated blind-play disconnects from the rank-3 clue, so Cathy can read the clue as this Bluff rather than as a connecting Finesse.

</TabItem>

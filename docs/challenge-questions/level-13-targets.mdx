<TabItem value="question">

At Level 13, Alice wants to initiate a Bluff on a completely untouched card. Which of these targets can be legal with a **number clue**: (a) any one-away-from-playable card, (b) any useful 3, (c) any critical non-5?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**(a) and (b), but not (c).** Ordinary one-away Bluff targets work with either clue type. The artificial 3 Bluff also works with either color or number. Critical non-5 cards are only added by Critical **Color** Bluff, so a number clue does not make an arbitrary critical non-5 a legal Bluff target.

</TabItem>

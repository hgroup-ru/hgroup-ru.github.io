<TabItem value="question">

There are 0 clues. Red 4 is already discarded. Alice has a known-playable blue 2. Bob has a critical red 4 on chop and no playable cards. Cathy's hand is Locked, all critical, and none of her cards is playable. Alice discards instead of playing blue 2, so Bob recognizes a Scream Discard and Chop Moves red 4. Scream rules normally forbid Bob from discarding now. What must Bob do instead of wasting the last clue?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob must make a **Rebellious Discard**. If he obeyed the ordinary post-Scream rule and spent a clue, Cathy would then have no clue and would be forced to discard a critical card. Bob therefore keeps the Scream's Chop Move, then **discards his new chop**, creating the clue Cathy needs. The second emergency discard is itself another Scream Discard.

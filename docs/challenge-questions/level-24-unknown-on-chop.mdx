<TabItem value="question">

The same Unnecessary advanced move instead uses **unknown trash on chop** as its clue target. Does the extra effect remain a Chop Move?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No. It becomes a Trash Push.** Once the focus is learned to be trash on chop, Level 24 treats the unnecessary extra message as if known trash had been clued on chop: the card immediately to its left/new chop is pushed to play. The off-chop version is the one that adds a Chop Move.

</TabItem>

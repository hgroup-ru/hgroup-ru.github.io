<TabItem value="question">

A re-clue adds no new positive clue mark to a card, but after the clue the receiver can narrow its superposition from {red 2, red 3} to exactly red 3 because the game state has advanced. Is this a No-Information clue?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** No-Information requires both no additional positive information **and the same set of possibilities before and after the clue sequence**. Narrowing the card's superposition is valuable information even without a new visible clue mark. Also, a genuinely No-Information clue may get a single card by Finesse, but a single-card No-Information Bluff is disallowed; a Bluff interpretation must get two cards.

</TabItem>

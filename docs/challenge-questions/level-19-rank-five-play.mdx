<TabItem value="question">

The game is in the Low Score Phase. A 5 is currently playable and sits two-or-more-away from chop. Alice wants to give it a direct Play Clue. May she use a **number 5** clue for that purpose?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** In the Low Score Phase, number-5 clues are never interpreted as direct Play Clues. If the team needs to directly Play Clue a playable 5 at this score, Alice must use a color clue or wait. A rank-5 clue instead enters the Level 19 special-move logic such as 5 Stall, 5's Chop Move, or 5 Pull.

</TabItem>

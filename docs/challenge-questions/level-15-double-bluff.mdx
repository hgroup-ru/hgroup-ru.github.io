Red 1 is played. Alice clues red to Donald's red 4. Bob blind-plays an unrelated blue 1. Cathy sees that red 4 is neither trash nor a legal single-Bluff target, and her Finesse Position is yellow 1. What should Cathy do?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Cathy should **immediately blind-play yellow 1**. The first blind-play cannot explain a red-4 Play Clue as an ordinary Bluff, so the clue points to a **Double Bluff**. Both blind-plays must happen on the two turns directly following the clue.

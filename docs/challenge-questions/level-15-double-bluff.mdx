<TabItem value="question">

Alice gives a Play Clue from Bluff Seat to a useful card that is **not** a legal single-Bluff target. Bob blind-plays successfully, and Cathy also has a playable Finesse Position. What must happen for the clue to resolve as a Double Bluff?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob and Cathy must both blind-play on the **two turns immediately following the clue**. A Double Bluff is used when a single Bluff cannot legally explain the target, and it resolves after exactly two blind-plays. Like other Bluff-style moves it is Bluff Seat-only and immediate; the team does not queue it for later turns.

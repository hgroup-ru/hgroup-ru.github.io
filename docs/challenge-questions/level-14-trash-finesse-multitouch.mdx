<TabItem value="question">

Only red 1 and blue 1 remain unplayed. Alice clues number 1 to Donald, newly touching two green 1s. Bob sees red 1 on Cathy's Finesse Position. Because two cards were touched, does Bob also need to blind-play blue 1 as a second connection?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Double Trash Finesses are explicitly disallowed. A Trash Finesse only promises **one** matching card; seeing Cathy's red 1 is enough for Bob to discard. After Cathy blind-plays red 1, Donald can conclude that **both newly touched cards are trash**, not that a second matching 1 is still promised.

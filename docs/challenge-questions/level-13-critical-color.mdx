<TabItem value="question">

Red 1 is played and one red 4 is already discarded. Alice gives Cathy a red Play Clue touching one previously untouched card. Bob blind-plays blue 1. Which two red identities must Cathy consider?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Cathy must consider **red 3 and red 4**. Red 3 is the normal one-away Bluff target. Because the remaining red 4 is critical, Level 13 also permits a Critical Color Bluff on it: the clue is a color clue, the card was previously untouched, and it is not a 5. The blind-play does not distinguish those two possibilities yet.

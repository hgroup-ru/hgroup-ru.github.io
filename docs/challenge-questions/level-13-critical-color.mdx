Red 1 is played and one red 4 is discarded. Alice gives Cathy a red Play Clue on one previously untouched card. Bob blind-plays blue 1. Which red identities must Cathy consider?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Cathy keeps **red 3 and red 4**. Red 3 is the ordinary one-away Bluff target. The remaining red 4 is critical, so it is also legal under Critical Color Bluff: the clue is a color clue, the card was completely untouched, and the target is not a 5.

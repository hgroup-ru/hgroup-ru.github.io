<TabItem value="question">

Red 2 and blue 2 are played. Bob has blue-3 Elimination Notes on slots 3, 4, and 5. Alice clues Bob's slot-1 4 as a Self-Finesse. If the 4 could be red 4 with a normal red-3 Finesse from slot 2, or blue 4 with a blue-3 Self-Elimination Finesse from slot 5, which interpretation does Bob take?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob takes the **normal Finesse** and blind-plays slot 2 as red 3. Normal Finesses have priority over Self-Elimination Finesses, so Bob must not jump to the older elimination candidate merely because that alternative also fits.

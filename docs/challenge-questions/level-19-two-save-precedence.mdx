<TabItem value="question">

Early Game, Low Score Phase. Alice gives Bob a rank-5 clue. From Bob's perspective the only other thing Alice might have needed to do was a 2 Save on Cathy's chop, and that 2 Save would be impossible if Bob himself holds the other copy of the 2. Should Bob force a 5 Pull interpretation?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No. Prefer the 5 Stall.** In this specific interaction, if the only ignored action is a 2 Save on chop, the simple 5 Stall takes precedence over 5 Pull. Bob should account for the possibility that Alice was blocked from the 2 Save by the Visible Rule and mark his own hand for the other copy rather than blind-playing a pulled card.

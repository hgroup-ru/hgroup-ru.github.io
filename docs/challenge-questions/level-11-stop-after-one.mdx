<TabItem value="question">

All 2s were played. Alice, who is in Bluff Seat for Bob, gave Cathy a red Play Clue on her red 4.

**Alice's clue:**

<Clue />

Bob immediately blind-played his Finesse Position expecting red 3; it was the blue 3 and played successfully. Bob's newly drawn Finesse Position really is the red 3.

**After Bob's blind-play:**

<Question />

Should Bob continue digging for the red 3 as a Layered Finesse?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** From Bluff Seat, when the immediate unrelated blind-play can form a legal Bluff, the Bluff interpretation takes precedence over a Layered Finesse. Red 4 was a legal one-away-from-playable Bluff target, and blue 3 disconnected from the red clue, so the misinformation was immediately resolved as a Bluff. Bob is no longer promised another blind-play just because the next physical card happens to be red 3.

</TabItem>

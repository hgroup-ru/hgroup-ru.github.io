<TabItem value="question">

Alice performs an Unnecessary Unknown Trash Discharge using unknown trash off Donald's chop. Bob completes the normal Discharge blind-play. Cathy can now see that Alice could have directly Play Clued Bob's card, so the extra Level 24 meaning would normally Chop Move Donald's cards to the right of the clue target. Cathy also sees that the card this would Chop Move is already trash.

Should Cathy simply let Donald perform that bad Chop Move?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Cathy recognizes the impending trash Chop Move as a **Bad Chop Move Ejection** and blind-plays the appropriate Finesse Position to eject the useful card instead. Level 24's extra Chop Move is still subject to the existing protection against knowingly Chop Moving trash; the unnecessary-move interpretation can therefore enter an Ejection chain rather than ending at the first Chop Move.

Red 2 is played. Alice gives Cathy a red clue that newly touches an unclued red 5. Bob sees red 3 and red 4 as the two cards he would need to blind-play to complete the ordinary Finesse. Which position should Bob play instead?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob should blind-play his **Second Finesse Position**. Because the normal Finesse would require two blind-plays, the clue is interpreted as a **5 Color Ejection**. Prompts do not count toward this threshold; only blind-plays do.

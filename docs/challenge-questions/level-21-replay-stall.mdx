<TabItem value="question">

Alice re-clues a globally known playable card without introducing any new card. She is in a valid stalling situation and cannot safely discard. Must the team interpret the clue as Replay Double Ignition?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** The ordinary reason for replaying a known playable card is precisely a legal stall. Replay Double Ignition only follows when the re-clue is otherwise useless **and Alice is not in a stalling situation**. The RDI also must not introduce new cards.

</TabItem>

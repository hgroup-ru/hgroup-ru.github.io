<TabItem value="question">

Cathy receives a valid 5 Pull. The pulled slot 2 could be red 2, but an already-clued red 1 elsewhere has not played yet. Must Cathy immediately blind-play slot 2 as though she were Finessed?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A 5 Pull is treated as a **Delayed Play Clue or potential Prompt**, not as a normal Finesse. The pulled card is allowed to connect through existing touched cards, so Cathy can wait for the known red 1 to play before playing the pulled red 2.

<TabItem value="question">

A UDD clue newly touched exactly two cards in Cathy's hand, and she can deduce that both must be the same duplicated red 2. Slot 2 is the focus and slot 3 is the other copy. Must Cathy still discard the focus first?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Not necessarily.** When exactly two new cards were touched and Cathy knows they are the same card, she may discard the **non-focused copy** instead to cause a **Trash Order Chop Move**. This is the explicit exception to the normal focus-first UDD discard rule.

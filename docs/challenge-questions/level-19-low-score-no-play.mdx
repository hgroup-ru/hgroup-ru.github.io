In a normal five-suit game the score is 9. Red 4 is played, and Cathy's red 5 is two-or-more-away from chop. Alice wants to tell Cathy that red 5 is directly playable. May Alice use a number-5 clue as an ordinary Play Clue?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** A score of 9 is still in the **Low Score Phase** because the five-suit threshold is 10. During that phase, number-5 clues are never direct Play Clues. Alice must use a color clue or wait until the Normal Score Phase; a rank-5 clue must be interpreted through the Level 19 5-tech rules instead.

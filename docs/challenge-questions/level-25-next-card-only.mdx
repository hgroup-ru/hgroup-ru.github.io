<TabItem value="question">

Alice has known playable blue 3 and red 3. Bob has known blue 4. Cathy has known red 5, but no one holds a known red 4 as the immediate next card. Looking far ahead, the red line could eventually reach Cathy's red 5, while blue 3 immediately leads into Bob's blue 4. No player is Locked.

Which play gets the "leads into someone else's hand" Priority?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Blue 3.** When deciding whether a card leads into someone else's hand, Priority considers only the **very next card**. Blue 3 immediately leads to Bob's blue 4; red 3 does not immediately lead to a teammate merely because a later red 5 exists. Longer future chains are ignored for this comparison.

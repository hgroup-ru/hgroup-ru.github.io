<TabItem value="question">

Red 1 is played. Bob visibly holds an unclued red 2 and red 3, neither of them in Finesse Position. Cathy has an unclued red 4 on chop. Alice could give Cathy a direct red clue touching only the 4.

Is Cathy's red 4 Delayed Playable, and may Alice give that Play Clue now?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Cathy's red 4 is **Phantom Playable**, not Delayed Playable. It is playable only through visible connectors that are neither clued nor in Finesse Position. Alice therefore may **not** give the red 4 a Play Clue yet; the in-between red 2 and red 3 have to be clued first.

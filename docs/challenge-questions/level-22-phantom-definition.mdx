<TabItem value="question">

A card would become playable after two visible connecting cards in another player's hand. Those connectors are not clued and are not on Finesse Position. Is the target merely Delayed Playable, or Phantom Playable? May the team give it a Play Clue immediately?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

It is **Phantom Playable**. Delayed Playable cards are playable through already clued cards or through cards on Finesse Position. Here the connectors are only visible in a hand, so the target is playable only in a phantom sense. The team **must not give it a Play Clue yet**; the in-between cards have to be clued first.

</TabItem>

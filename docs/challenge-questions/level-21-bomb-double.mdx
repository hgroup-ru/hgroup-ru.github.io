<TabItem value="question">

It is Mid-Game, well before the last two rounds. Alice has a globally known-trash card and could discard it safely. Instead, she deliberately misplays that trash card. Bob and Cathy both have playable cards in Finesse Position.

How should Bob and Cathy react?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

They should treat the unnecessary strike as a **Bomb Double Ignition** and both blind-play their Finesse Position cards. Near the very end, intentionally misplaying known trash can instead be a Positional Misplay that communicates a slot number; doing it this early has no such ordinary purpose, so the wasted strike carries the Double Ignition message.

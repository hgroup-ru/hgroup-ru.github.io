<TabItem value="question">

A player has a globally known-trash card. Well before the last or second-to-last round, they intentionally misplay that trash card instead of safely discarding it. What does Level 21 assign to this otherwise useless strike expenditure?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

It triggers a **Bomb Double Ignition**: two teammates blind-play from Finesse Position. Near the very end, intentionally misplaying known trash can instead be used as a Positional Misplay to communicate a slot number; the Level 21 Double Ignition interpretation is motivated specifically by doing this earlier, when wasting a strike otherwise has no ordinary reason.

</TabItem>

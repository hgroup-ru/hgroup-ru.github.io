<TabItem value="question">

On turn 1, Alice clues Cathy red on one card. Bob blind-plays blue 1. Cathy's touched card is physically red 3. Which identities should Cathy keep?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Cathy keeps **red 2 or red 3**. Blue does not connect to the red clue, so this is a Bluff rather than a Finesse. Red 2 is the ordinary one-away target; red 3 is also legal under the Level 13 3 Bluff convention when it is two-away-from-playable.

On the first turn, Alice clued Cathy red, touching one card. Bob immediately blind-played a blue 1. Cathy's touched card is physically the red 3 shown in the diagram.

What possibilities should Cathy keep for the touched card?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Cathy should keep **red 2 or red 3**. The blue 1 disconnects from the red clue, so this is a Bluff rather than a Finesse. A normal Bluff can target the one-away red 2, while the Level 13 3 Bluff convention additionally allows the useful red 3. Nothing in this line distinguishes the two yet.

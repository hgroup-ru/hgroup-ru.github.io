<TabItem value="question">

A Trash Finesse clue newly touches **two** cards that the receiver would otherwise read as two promised playable cards. Cathy can already see one matching playable connector on another player's Finesse Position. Is a second connector promised for the second touched card?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Double Trash Finesses are explicitly disallowed. A Trash Finesse only promises the one matching blind-play needed to demonstrate the deception. Once that happens, **all newly touched cards** from the Trash Finesse are understood to be trash. The second touched card does not demand a second connector.

</TabItem>

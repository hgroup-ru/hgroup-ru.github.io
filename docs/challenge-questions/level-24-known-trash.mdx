<TabItem value="question">

An advanced move is clearly Unnecessary and uses **known trash** as its clue target. What category of extra effect does Level 24 add? Could Alice instead encode the extra message as an Unnecessary Trash Chop Move?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Known trash adds an **Ignition**. An Unnecessary Trash Chop Move is explicitly **illegal**, because it would be indistinguishable from Chop Move Ignition. The normal Ignition is on Bob, subject to the two stated target-shifting exceptions when Bob already has a known play or is himself the clue receiver blind-playing from the clue.

</TabItem>

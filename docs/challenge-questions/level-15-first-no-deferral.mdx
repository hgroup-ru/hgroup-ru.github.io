<TabItem value="question">

Alice clues red to Donald's red 4 in a position where Bob can recognize that a Double Bluff is possible. Bob also sees a very attractive Finesse clue he could give before responding. May Bob defer his blind-play for one turn to give that clue first?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** If the first blind-player suspects a **Double Bluff**, they should **never defer**, even to perform another Finesse or Bluff. Double Bluffs must resolve immediately or the recipients' interpretations desynchronize.

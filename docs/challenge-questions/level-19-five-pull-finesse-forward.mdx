<TabItem value="question">

On the first turn Alice clues Cathy's slot-2 5. Cathy also has a playable yellow 1, so the clue cannot be a Stall, and Cathy's pulled slot 3 is red 2, one-away-from-playable. Bob is between Alice and Cathy. What must happen before Cathy's turn for the 5 Pull Finesse interpretation to be valid?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Bob must blind-play red 1 before Cathy's turn.** A 5 Pull Finesse must be demonstrated by a blind-play between the 5 clue and the pulled player's next turn. Consequently, a basic 5 Pull Finesse must be forward; it cannot directly initiate a Reverse Finesse that leaves Cathy guessing through her own turn.

<TabItem value="question">

Can a genuine No-Information re-clue cause a No-Information Finesse or Bluff when the touched card is already playable? And can the same principle ever produce a Layered Finesse on the next player?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

The touched card must be **one-or-more-away-from-playable**; an already-playable touched card does not satisfy this Level 20 condition. The principle **can** produce a No-Information Layered Finesse on the next player. In that case the next player distinguishes it from a Bluff because some other more-useful clue could have initiated the Bluff instead.

</TabItem>

<TabItem value="question">

Nothing is played. Cathy's red 2 was already red-clued, and Alice gives the same red clue again; by stipulation, the card's superposition is unchanged, so this is a genuine No-Information re-clue. Cathy also has an untouched blue 2 that Alice could have clued normally if she merely wanted to initiate a Bluff. Bob's Finesse Position is green 1 and his Second Finesse Position is red 1. What should Bob do?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Bob should treat the re-clue as a **No-Information Layered Finesse**. He blind-plays green 1 first; after that unrelated playable card succeeds, he continues through the layer and later blind-plays red 1 to connect Cathy's red 2.

The touched red 2 is one-away-from-playable, satisfying the Level 20 distance condition. Bob distinguishes this from a Bluff because Alice had the more useful untouched blue-2 clue available if a Bluff were all she wanted. An already-playable touched card could not be used for a No-Information Finesse or Bluff.

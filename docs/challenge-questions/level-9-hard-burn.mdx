<TabItem value="question">

It is the Early Game. Alice has no normal Play Clue or Save Clue and no available 5 Stall. Bob's blue 5 is already identified, so re-cluing number 5 would add absolutely no information: a Hard Burn.

May Alice use that Hard Burn as an Early Game Stall?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Hard Burn is the lowest-precedence Stall-table option and is not allowed in the Early Game. It becomes available only in the more severe Double Discard / Scream Discard, Locked Hand, and 8-clue Stalling Situations, and only after every higher-precedence available clue has been exhausted.

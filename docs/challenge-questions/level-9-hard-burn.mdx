<TabItem value="question">

It is the Early Game. Alice has no normal Play Clue or Save Clue and no available 5 Stall. Bob's blue 5 already has enough information for Bob to know exactly what it is. Alice considers giving Bob another number 5 clue so that she does not have to make the first unknown discard.

Is that a legal Early Game stall?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** The re-clue adds no information, so it is a **Hard Burn**. Hard Burn is not an Early Game Stall; it is reserved for more severe Stalling Situations and sits below the other allowed stall clues in precedence. Alice cannot manufacture an extra Early Game action by re-cluing a card that Bob already knows exactly.

<TabItem value="question">

Alice is in the Early Game with no normal Play Clue or Save Clue and no 5 Stall. She could re-clue a card in a way that gives absolutely no new information: a Hard Burn.

May she use the Hard Burn as an Early Game Stall?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**No.** Hard Burn is the lowest-precedence Stall-table option and is not allowed in the Early Game. It becomes available only in the more severe Double Discard / Scream Discard, Locked Hand, and 8-clue Stalling Situations, and only after every higher-precedence available clue has been exhausted.

</TabItem>

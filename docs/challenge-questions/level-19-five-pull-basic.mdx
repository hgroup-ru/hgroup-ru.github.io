<TabItem value="question">

It is the first turn. Alice has another Play Clue available, so her number-5 clue to Cathy's slot-1 red 5 cannot be a 5 Stall. The card immediately to the right, slot 2, is a playable blue 1. Bob initially notices that red 5 would normally need a red-4 Finesse. Who should blind-play, and which card?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Cathy blind-plays slot 2, the blue 1.** In the Low Score Phase the number-5 clue is not a Play Clue on red 5, so it does not create the apparent red-4 Finesse. It is a **5 Pull**, which gets the card immediately to the right of the clued 5 and takes precedence over that Finesse interpretation.

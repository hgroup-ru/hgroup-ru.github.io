<TabItem value="question">

Red 1 is played and the team is out of clues. Alice knows her clued red card is `r3`. Bob has a playable `g1` in slot 1 and the other `r3` in slot 2.

Can Alice discard her known `r3`, intending to transfer it to Bob?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

No. Discarding the known `r3` with the intention of transferring it to Bob would be a Layered Baton Discard, which is explicitly forbidden: a Baton target must be exactly on the current Finesse Position. The playable `g1` in front of Bob's `r3` does not make a layered Baton legal.

</TabItem>

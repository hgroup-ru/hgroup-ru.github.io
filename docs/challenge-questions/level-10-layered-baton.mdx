<TabItem value="question">

Red 1 is played. Alice knows her clued red card is `r3`. Bob has a playable `g1` in slot 1 and the other `r3` in slot 2.

May Alice discard `r3` to transfer it to Bob?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

No. That would be a **Layered Baton Discard**, which is explicitly forbidden. A Baton target must be exactly on the current Finesse Position; unlike a Gentleman's Discard, Baton cannot use a playable layer in front of the target.

<TabItem value="question">

Red 1 is played and the team is out of clues. Alice knows her clued red card is `r3`. Bob has a playable `g1` in slot 1 and the other `r3` in slot 2. Alice has no other known safe discard.

How should Alice move?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

Alice should **make an ordinary chop discard**, not discard the known `r3`. Using `r3` as a transfer would be a Layered Baton Discard, which is explicitly forbidden: a Baton target must be exactly on the current Finesse Position. The playable `g1` in front of Bob's `r3` does not make a layered Baton legal.

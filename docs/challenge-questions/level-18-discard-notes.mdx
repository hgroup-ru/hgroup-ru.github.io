Red 3 is played. Alice unexpectedly discards a playable red 4 from chop, and the team was not busy. She therefore concludes that the other red 4 must already have been in her hand. After drawing a new slot-1 card, which slots get red-4 Elimination Notes?

<Question />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Slots 2, 3, 4, and 5.** The newly drawn slot 1 cannot be the copy whose existence explained why the team allowed the discard. Discard Elimination records the possible locations that were already in Alice's hand when the red 4 was lost.

<TabItem value="question">

It is the Early Game with 3 clues. Bob has three cards that were saved earlier with a 2 Save, and he has not discarded since. Cathy has a red 2 on chop. Alice now makes the first completely unknown discard, ending the Early Game.

Which players should now mark Bob's three clued 2s as possible red 2s?

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="solution">

**Alice, Bob, and Donald should; Cathy should not.** Alice's discard says there was no remaining required clue, so the visible red 2 on Cathy's chop cannot be the only red 2 outside Bob's hand. Because Bob has not discarded since his 2 Save, Alice, Bob, and Donald can infer that one of Bob's three clued 2s is the other red 2 and mark all three as possible red 2.

Cathy cannot see her own chop card, so she cannot reconstruct that deduction. This is the asymmetric-information desync from the Level 12 source: the team must remember that Cathy does **not** share those notes.

</TabItem>
